## Description (4000 char max)

```
TERRA IMPERIUM is a grand strategy game spanning 4,300 years on a real Earth globe — every one of
240 nations is playable, from the Bronze Age to the Space Age.

CHOOSE YOUR NATION
Any country, any era. Start in 2000 BCE and guide your people through five ages of history —
Bronze, Classical, Kingdoms, Gunpowder, and the Modern Age — all the way to 2300 CE.

A LIVING WORLD, NOT A BACKDROP
Every one of Earth's 240 nations is a real opponent with its own personality, economy, and army.
Empires rise, collapse, and get conquered — including yours, if you're not careful.

BUILD A REAL ARMY
Recruit infantry, cavalry, ranged troops, siege engines, and navies with a genuine rock-paper-
scissors counter system. Promote veteran units through the ranks, appoint generals with their own
traits, and manage supply lines that can win or lose a war before a single battle is fought.

GOVERN A NATION, NOT JUST AN ARMY
Manage resources that evolve with the ages — Gold and manpower always matter, then Copper, Iron,
and Oil as your empire advances. Build infrastructure, quell unrest, adopt governments and
policies, and balance guns against butter.

DIPLOMACY WITH TEETH
Declare wars with real justification, forge alliances, fabricate claims, vassalize the weak, and
watch the world's other powers form coalitions against whoever's winning too fast — including you.

REACH FOR THE STARS
Push into the Modern Age's space race: launch satellites, build missile arsenals, and race the
world to the Moon, Mars, and beyond for resources no empire can extract any other way.

HISTORY REACTS TO YOU
Live through world-shaping events — plagues, gold rushes, world wars — that land differently
depending on your situation, plus your own nation's unique story if you're one of history's great
powers.

FIVE WAYS TO WIN
Dominate the map, out-trade the world, out-diplomat every rival, win the space race, or simply
outlast everyone else to the year 2300 — your empire, your victory condition.

Every game is a different world. Every nation is a different story. The only constant is the map —
and it's the whole Earth.
```
