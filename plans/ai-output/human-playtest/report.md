# Human playtest: battle-commander, battles

# Playtest report: battle sandbox, battle-commander persona (phone 844x390, English)

## 1. Summary

The persona played eight age sessions over 56 steps.

**What worked:**
- The setup screen, the Fight button and the formation presets (Line, Wide) worked every time.
- Once a battle was live, the bottom bar, the Enemy camera jump, pinch zoom, the army bars and the result screen all read well.
- No page errors were caught and there were no harness-error lines. Every battle the player started did load.

**What fell short:**
- Seven of eight battles were started (stone-field never left deploy), but only two reached a result. Both were the sack defenses, and both ended in about 42 to 43 seconds with a HELD win.
- The other five sessions ran out of steps mid-battle. No field, walled or naval battle was seen to its end.
- Sieges, power effects and most of the economy were barely touched.

**The three things that matter most:**
1. **Manual deploy is broken or invisible.** Tapping a squad in the deploy phase never showed any selection, in five sessions. In stone-field the follow-up placement tap did not move the squad either, so no squad was ever placed by hand.
2. **Sack defenses end before the player can act.** The raid broke and withdrew in about 42 to 43 s, before a power could be used or the economy touched (steps 27, 56).
3. **Readability on a phone is weak.** Units are small at the default zoom, the top strip is crowded with small text (objective box in orange at about 9 px), and the objective box covers the walls and the player's own banners. Banners vanish when zoomed in.

## 4. Fun curve

**Baseline.** Fun sat at 3 for nearly every step and frustration at 1. The curve is flat: nothing went badly wrong after stone age, but nothing took off either.

**Sinks:**
- **Steps 4 to 7 (stone-field).** Frustration rose to 2, then to 3 at step 7, and fun fell to 2. This was the low point of the run: repeated taps on a squad gave no feedback and the session ended stuck in deploy.
- **Repeat of the same failure.** Frustration went back to 2 at steps 11, 18 and 32. Each time the persona hit the same deploy selection problem, gave up and pressed Start.
- **Steps 47 to 49 (information-naval).** Frustration was 2 when a destroyer was already lost and the box select caught only one ship.

**Peaks.** Fun reached 4 four times, all when the game gave clear, punchy feedback:
- Step 21: firing Ramming Speed as the fleets locked together.
- Step 26: "Enemy knights broke" and a 97% vs 66% lead.
- Step 40: zooming into detailed modern units.
- Step 55: "Enemy hover tanks broke" and a 99% vs 81% lead.

**Endings.** Each sack peak was followed straight away by frustration 2 (steps 27 and 56). The battle ended before the persona could build on the moment. The fun in this build comes from clarity moments, and the frustration from either the deploy dead end or battles that are cut short.

## 5. Top issues (ranked by harm to a real player)

### 1. Deploy phase: tapping a squad shows no selection, and manual placement never worked

**What happened.** Tapping a squad's figures or its banner gave no highlight, no ring and no panel change, in five sessions. In stone-field the follow-up tap inside the zone did not move the squad, so the player could not tell whether selection or placement had failed. Every later session gave up on manual placement and relied on presets.

**Steps:** 3 to 7, 10 to 11, 17 to 18, 31 to 32.

**Shot:** shots/step-005.jpg.

**Unclear:** the taps may have just missed small targets. But banner taps also failed, and selection rings do appear in live battle (13), so deploy looks like the odd one out.

**Fix:** show the same selection ring in deploy, and change the deploy panel to name the selected squad. Add a "tap a squad first" hint when a zone tap has nothing selected, and enlarge the tap target around banners.

### 2. Sack defenses end in about 42 to 43 seconds

**What happened.** The raiders broke and withdrew before the player could use a power, the economy or any tactics.

**Steps:** 24 to 27, 52 to 56.

**Shots:** shots/step-027.jpg.

**Caution:** real time passes while the model thinks between steps, so a human would get more actions into 42 s. Even so, the enemy breaking at 66% and at 81% suggests the raid is too weak to test anything. The end coming right after 2x was tapped (26) is likely coincidence, not a speed bug.

**Fix:** make the raid larger, or send it in waves. Alternatively, hold the first wave until the player has had a beat to act.

### 3. Small units and crowded small text on a phone

**What happened.**
- Units are small at the default zoom, and only banners and health bars read.
- The objective box, the housing chip and the speed buttons share the top strip. They cover the player's own banners and the city walls.
- The objective text is orange on dark at about 9 px.
- The result-screen footnotes are hard to read.

**Steps:** 5, 27, 28, 35, 40, 42, 53, 56.

**Shots:** shots/step-053.jpg, shots/step-040.jpg, shots/step-027.jpg.

**Fix:** use a slightly closer default zoom on phones. Make the objective box collapsible or move it off-center, and use at least 11 to 12 px text with stronger contrast.

### 4. Box select in naval caught only one ship

**What happened.** A drag over the upper ships in Select mode picked up a single destroyer.

**Steps:** 46 to 48.

**Shot:** none taken.

**Unclear:** this could be the drag path, ships moving in real time, or Select mode expecting a lasso rather than a box.

**Fix:** check box and lasso hit-testing for ships, and show a live outline while dragging so the player sees what will be caught.

### 5. Squad banners vanish when zoomed in

**What happened.** Zoomed in, the player cannot tell which squad is which.

**Step:** 40.

**Shot:** shots/step-040.jpg.

**Fix:** keep a small banner or number above each squad at all zoom levels.

### 6. Panels cover too much of the battlefield

**What happened.** The deploy panel covers about a third of the screen. The economy panel covers about half while open.

**Steps:** 7, 23, 35, 51.

**Shots:** none taken.

**Fix:** make the panels collapsible or semi-transparent, or narrow them on landscape phones.

### 7. Line preset in the sack deploy moved squads out of view

**What happened.** After applying Line, the squads were no longer on screen.

**Step:** 24.

**Shots:** none taken.

**Fix:** after a preset, pan the camera to frame the deployed squads.

### 8. Enemy walls and gate are hard to pick out from the terrain

**What happened.** The persona could not easily see the walls or the gate it meant to attack.

**Step:** 42.

**Shots:** none taken.

**Fix:** give walls and gates more contrast or an outline.

## 6. Bugs

No page errors and no harness-error lines were recorded.

1. **Deploy squad selection gives no feedback, and placement fails** (stone-field).
   1. Open the stone age open field sandbox and tap Fight (step 1).
   2. Tap the Line preset (step 2).
   3. Tap the ranged squad's figures at about 266,219, then its banner at about 282,218 (steps 3 and 4). No highlight or panel change appears.
   4. Tap inside the deploy zone at 180,260 (step 6). The squad does not move.
   5. This reproduced in bronze (10), classical-naval (17) and gunpowder (31).

2. **Laborer cost mismatch** (gunpowder-field).
   1. Start the gunpowder open field battle (steps 29 to 32).
   2. Open Economy (33).
   3. Tap Laborer, which is labeled 50 food (34).
   4. Population goes 17 to 18, but 80 food is spent (35).
   5. Unclear: food may have changed for other reasons in real time during the step. Worth checking.

3. **Population line truncated** with an ellipsis in the Economy panel (step 35, same path as bug 2).

4. **Setup pickers show "mixed terrain" for a link-configured sea battle.**
   1. Open the classical naval sandbox link. The pickers show mixed terrain (step 15).
   2. Tap Fight. The battle loads as naval, so the pickers do not reflect the actual setup.

5. **Line preset moves squads off-screen in the sack deploy.**
   1. Open the kingdoms sack setup and tap Fight (step 22).
   2. Tap Line on the left-side panel at 159,158 (step 23).
   3. The squads move up out of view (24).

## 7. What was fun, and what else was good

**Fun moments:**
- Ramming Speed as the fleets locked together (21).
- The rout toasts "Enemy knights broke" and "Enemy hover tanks broke" with the army bar swinging (26, 55).
- Zooming in on detailed modern tanks, infantry and trucks (40).

**Other good things:**
- The setup screen is clean, and Fight is always in the same easy spot.
- The Line and Wide presets work instantly and visibly (2, 16, 44).
- In battle, All plus selection rings and the Attack mode hint are clear (13, 14).
- The Enemy camera jump finds the raiders immediately (25, 54). Pinch zoom is smooth (39, 41).
- The army and base bars and the objective box explain the win condition well, including "killing their army alone does not end it" (42).
- Reinforcements arriving (8 to 10) and the Idle button were clear (42).
- The result screen is well organized: HELD, losses as bars, XP per regiment, general status, and houses ruined with free repairs (27, 56).
- The bottom bar buttons, the speed buttons, the powers button and Continue are all finger-sized. The English is clear throughout.
