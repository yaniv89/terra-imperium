# Human playtest: warmonger

# Playtest Report: Warmonger Persona, Campaign, Phone 844x390

## 1. Summary

A warmonger persona played 247 steps across four sessions: start, classical, gunpowder and modern. The start session played Akkad. The classical, gunpowder and modern sessions appear to be prepared saves of a Tupinamba game already at war with Tiwanaku; the city names change and step 247 says "as Tupinamba".

The basic flows worked smoothly: setup, tutorial, saving, event choices and queuing builds. The core fantasy for this persona never happened. The player was formally at war for the whole classical, gunpowder and modern stretch but never found the enemy, never launched an attack, and fought only one battle: a defence of 400 militia against 40,000 attackers that was lost in seconds. Fun sat at 3 for almost the entire run and never went higher. Most steps went to the same open city, scroll, queue, close, close loop. No page errors were caught.

**The three things that matter most:**
1. **Being at war gives the player no path to a fight.** There was no visible enemy front, and in the classical session no move or attack command the player could find on the army card (steps 63 to 124, 247).
2. **The per-city "Choose production" loop eats the game.** In the modern session about 50 of 61 steps were production handling, and closing each city takes two taps (steps 125 to 147, 186 to 246).
3. **Military build times are very long while gold piles up unused.** Warband 22 turns, musketeers 23, rifle infantry 28 to 34, tanks up to 51, all while the player held 11k gold (steps 51, 172, 190, 246, 247).

## 3. Fun curve

- **Steps 1 to 30: fun 3, frustration 1.** This was smooth onboarding. Setup, tutorial, first techs, the Warlord virtue and founding Kish all went without friction. It was never exciting, but nothing hurt.
- **Steps 31 to 50: frustration rises to 2.** The player tried to declare war (steps 30 to 31) and learned it needed a cause and 300 gold. Several empty end turns followed (steps 39 to 41): "Slow so far."
- **Steps 51 to 54: first low point, fun 2, frustration 3.** Warband and Siege train entries were greyed out behind Copper Working with a 22-turn cost (step 51). A conqueror who cannot build an army is the worst possible early message.
- **Steps 55 to 62: recovery to 3/2.** Copper Working finished and the army marched toward the Karkheh. This was the closest the run came to "going somewhere."
- **Steps 63 to 88: frustration back to 3 at 67 to 68 and 84 to 88.** The player was at war with Tiwanaku but could not find the front or a move/attack command (notes 67, 84, 85).
- **Steps 89 to 175: flat 3/2 plateau.** This stretch was dominated by production loops and quiet end turns. The player kept writing "hoping for contact" (steps 110, 115, 116, 118, 151, 158, 159).
- **Steps 176 to 185: second low point, fun 2, frustration 3.** The first and only battle arrived (step 176) but was 400 against 40,000. The army hit 0 within seconds (step 183).
- **Steps 186 to 247: flat 3/2 to the end.** This was mostly production prompts, with ten turns played and no battle.

**Peak:** there is no real peak. Fun never exceeded 3. The most hopeful moments were marching toward the Karkheh (steps 57 to 59) and the start of the siege battle (steps 176 to 179), before the result was clear.

**Trough:** steps 51 to 54 and 183 to 185. Both are moments where the persona's goal (an army, then a fight) was blocked or crushed.

## 4. Top issues (ranked by harm to a real player)

### 1. War without contact: no way to find or reach the enemy
- **What happened:** The player was at war with Tiwanaku from the start of the classical session. Over about 15 end turns in classical and about 10 in modern, no enemy appeared. The player could not tell where the front was. The army card showed no visible move or attack command, and the card covers much of the phone map. In the start session the player did march an army (steps 57 to 58), so a move order exists. Either it is hidden or missing in some contexts, or the player missed it on the larger army card. The diary cannot tell which.
- **Steps:** 63 to 68, 75 to 77, 83 to 87, 110 to 119, 151 to 159, 218 to 243, summary at 124 and 247.
- **Screenshots:** shots/step-067.jpg, shots/step-084.jpg, shots/step-085.jpg.
- **Suggested fix:**
  - Add a "Go to war front" or "Show enemy" button to the war entry, with a camera jump to the nearest known enemy city or army.
  - Mark enemy territory on the map, even when fogged, once war is declared.
  - Give the army card a large, obvious Move / Attack button and let it collapse on phone screens.
  - If the enemy is unreachable (another continent, no contact), say so and point to naval transport or allies.

### 2. Per-city "Choose production" loop and double close
- **What happened:** Each city repeatedly asked for production. Each one took: open the prompt, scroll the list (the military unit is below the fold), tap queue, close the city card, then close a tile card underneath. That is five taps per city. In the modern session only about 10 turns were played in 61 steps. The prompts also seem to block or hide End Turn: at step 193 the button "seems hidden", and steps 201 and 206 say "to clear the prompt so I can end turn". The step 124 summary mentions a stack of side prompts (Ruler, Empire, Cities) staying open.
- **Steps:** 125 to 147, 153 to 156, 165 to 173, 188 to 215, 219 to 224, 230 to 240, 244 to 246.
- **Screenshots:** none attached.
- **Suggested fix:**
  - Add a "repeat last build" or standing order option per city.
  - Add an "apply to all cities" queue option.
  - Remember the scroll position or filter (for example Armies) in the build list.
  - Close the whole stack with one tap instead of city card then tile card.
  - Never let a production prompt hide End Turn; offer "end turn anyway."

### 3. Military build times feel far too long, and gold has no outlet
- **What happened:** Unit build times were 22 turns for the Warband, 23 for musketeers, 28 to 34 for rifle infantry and up to 51 for tanks. Swordsmen cost 412. Meanwhile the player repeatedly noted plentiful gold, reaching 11k by the modern session. For a warmonger, waiting 30 or more turns per unit with idle gold kills momentum.
- **Steps:** 51, 72, 172, 190, 246, summary at 247.
- **Screenshots:** shots/step-051.jpg.
- **Suggested fix:** Allow buying or rushing units with gold, or show that the option exists if it does. Review military production costs against city output in later eras. Show the projected army size when the war is declared.

### 4. Hopeless siege battle offered as "command it yourself"
- **What happened:** The game offered the player command of the Itaparica defence: 400 militia against 40,000. The army fell to 0 within seconds, the laborers died, and the town hall was at 848/1500. There was little the player could order. The player could not tell whether the fight was winnable.
- **Steps:** 176 to 185.
- **Screenshots:** shots/step-183.jpg.
- **Suggested fix:**
  - Show win odds or a clear "this battle cannot be won, aim: survive X minutes / evacuate" goal before deployment.
  - Offer auto-resolve as the recommended choice for extreme mismatches.
  - Give defenders meaningful tools such as walls, towers, a retreat or evacuation option, and repair that matters.
  - Also, the only enemy activity the war produced was this overwhelming strike. It deserves warning beforehand, such as a scout report that an army is approaching.

### 5. Early military locked with confusing wording
- **What happened:** The Warband and Siege train entries had greyed plus buttons. The text said the unit "does not exist in this age or needs Copper Working" while Copper Working was being researched. Tapping the entry did nothing (steps 47 to 48). The lock itself may be intended. The problem is the wording and the lack of a "ready in N turns" hint.
- **Steps:** 45 to 54.
- **Screenshots:** shots/step-051.jpg.
- **Suggested fix:** Show "Unlocks with Copper Working (researching, N turns left)" with a link to the tech. Allow pre-queuing so the build starts when the tech lands.

### 6. Battle start obstructions
- **What happened:** The Begin defence button was partly covered by a banner (step 177). Skipping the battle tutorial hints took two separate taps (steps 180 to 181) and the player called it fiddly. In the middle of an urgent siege, every tap counts.
- **Steps:** 177, 180 to 181, summary at 185.
- **Screenshots:** none attached.
- **Suggested fix:** Suppress banners during the battle entry flow. Add a single "Skip all hints" control.

### 7. Banner needs a second tap to close
- **What happened:** The home screen banner stayed after the first tap in two separate sessions. The second tap closed it.
- **Steps:** 63 to 64, 186 to 187.
- **Screenshots:** none attached.
- **Suggested fix:** Check the hit area of the banner close control on phone layout. This may be a slightly missed tap, so verify first.

## 5. Bugs

All of these come from the diary only. Several may be missed taps or intended behaviour, as noted.

1. **Banner does not dismiss on first tap** (possible hit-area problem, possibly a missed tap)
   - Load the classical or modern session save.
   - Tap the banner close at about (586, 72).
   - The banner stays. Tapping again at about (587, 72) closes it. Seen at steps 63 to 64 and 186 to 187.

2. **End Turn hidden while production prompts and city/tile cards are open**
   - Load the modern session.
   - Open a city production prompt (596, 346), queue a unit, and close the city card (384, 69).
   - Tap End Turn at (667, 347).
   - At step 193 the player reported the End Turn button seemed hidden. Two more close taps (steps 194 to 195) and clearing every city prompt were needed before End Turn worked at step 216. Unclear whether this is intended gating or a layering bug.

3. **Closing a city card reveals a second tile card underneath**
   - Open any city production from the prompt and queue a unit.
   - Tap close at (384, 69).
   - A tile card remains and needs a separate close at (321, 80). This repeats about 30 times across steps 128 to 240. It is likely a UX layering issue rather than a crash bug.

4. **Side prompts stay stacked** (from the step 124 summary)
   - Play several turns in the classical session.
   - The Ruler, Empire and Cities prompts stay open together. No specific reproduction steps were logged.

5. **Battle Begin button partly covered by a banner**
   - Load the gunpowder session and end turns until the Itaparica siege prompt (around step 176).
   - Choose to command the defence (132, 213).
   - The Begin button at about (430, 335) is partly covered by a banner (step 177).

6. **Possible: no move or attack command on the army card in the classical session**
   - Load the classical session.
   - Tap the army marker (about 419, 345).
   - The army card shows an upgrade option (step 76) but no visible move or attack command (step 84). The start session's march at steps 57 to 58 shows the command exists, so this may be discoverability rather than a bug. Check the screenshot at shots/step-084.jpg.

**Harness observation (not a game bug):** The gunpowder session (step 150) and the modern session (step 217) both report "play turn 152". The two sessions may have loaded saves with the same turn number, or the era labels may not match the saves. The harness setup should be checked.

## 6. What was good

- **Onboarding is clean.** World setup took four taps and the tutorial clicked through without confusion (steps 1 to 11).
- **Choices fit the persona.** Hunting Bands, Copper Working, the Warlord virtue and the manpower-flavoured event choices let the player express "I am a conqueror" early (steps 12 to 16, 25, 33, 60, 149). The Warlord confirm dialog showed its drawback clearly (step 104).
- **The declare-war dialog is clear about cost.** It showed 300 gold and the need for a cause, so the player knew why not to proceed (step 31).
- **Saving is reliable.** Manual save worked in two sessions (steps 35, 79) and autosave was noticed and appreciated (steps 114, 242).
- **The glossary has nice flavour.** History text about the player's own people was readable and liked (step 81, shots/step-081.jpg).
- **Ending a turn is fast** (step 247), and the Research link made the tank lock clear (step 247).
- **The battle entry flow works end to end.** Command choice, deploy, start, select all and Repair were all reachable (steps 176 to 184).
- No page errors were caught across 247 steps.

**Next run should aim at:**
1. Start or load a game with a **visible, land-adjacent neighbour**. Declare war with a cause, march an army into their territory and **fight an offensive RTS battle and siege**, giving real orders.
2. **Naval:** build ships, transport an army, and see whether this is how to reach Tiwanaku.
3. **Load** a manual save and confirm state is restored.
4. Test whether gold can **rush or buy** units, and whether any standing order or "repeat build" exists to avoid the production loop.
5. Try **peace talks** after a war, and look for World Congress and raids in the mid to late eras.
6. On the phone layout, check the army card's **Move / Attack** controls and the banner close hit area.
