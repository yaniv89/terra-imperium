# Human playtest: battle-commander, battles

# Playtest report: battle sandbox, battle-commander persona, phone 844x390 (English)

## 1. Summary

The model, playing as an RTS veteran, ran eight sandbox battles, one per age, over 207 steps. Every battle started. There were no harness errors and no page errors. Only three battles reached a result screen:

- **Kingdoms sack:** held, a win.
- **Future sack:** held, a win.
- **Modern walls:** defeat.

Results for the other sessions:

- **Stone field, bronze walls, gunpowder field:** still running when the session ended, with no clear way to finish.
- **Both naval battles (classical, information):** never reached a fight.

Fun never rose above 3 out of 5 at any step. Frustration peaked at 4 in both naval sessions. Some things worked well: the land controls (select all, attack-move, formations, Rally), the powers, the minimap jump and the result screens. The experience fell down on readability, pacing and naval.

**The three things that matter most:**

1. **Naval battles are unplayable as seen.** The sea is drawn as grass and stone road, ships sit on land and the fleets never meet (#78 to #96, #171 to #197).
2. **Land battles drag with no visible goal.** Field and wall battles ran for many minutes at 3x to 4x with no end in sight and no sense of who was winning (#29 to #37, #58 to #74, #118 to #141).
3. **The fight is hard to read.** Units are tiny, hidden under tree canopy and merged into blobs. Health bars are thin, powers give no visible effect, and the army was wiped with little warning (#16, #19, #113, #117, #152, #168).

## 4. Fun curve

**Fun was flat at 3 from #1 to #81.** Frustration sat at 1 to 2 through setup and the first orders. It rose to 3 at #16, when the player found the units unreadable, and stayed there through powers and economy. It dipped back to 2 for the economy and Map checks (#21 to #34), then returned to 3 at #35 to #37 as the stone battle refused to end. In bronze walls, frustration held at 3 from #48 onward because of the clipped power menu, the stuck speed button and slow progress.

**First sink: classical naval.** Fun dropped to 2 for the first time at #82, when the map overview showed "a tiny pond and mostly land". From there fun swung between 2 and 3, and frustration climbed to 4 at #91 to #96 as the camera kept drifting and the ships never moved. This is the low point of the run.

**Recovery: kingdoms sack.** Frustration fell to 1 at #102 and #103 because the result screen was clear. Fun stayed at 3: the battle ended before the player had really acted.

**Gunpowder field.** Frustration started low (1 at #105, #106, #108, #109) and crept to 3 by #129 to #132 as the melee ground on at 3x. It eased to 2 when the minimap jump worked (#135) and ended at 3.

**Modern walls.** Fun fell to 2 at #152 (the army stalled), recovered to 3 at #154 when the city was readable, and fell to 2 again at #167 to #170 (wipe and defeat).

**Second sink: information naval.** Fun was at 2 for most steps from #179, and frustration reached 4 at #195 to #197.

**Best stretch: future sack.** Frustration was 1 for most of #198 to #207, the lowest of the run.

**Peak:** fun never went above 3. The calmest moments were the sack defenses and the result screens, which suggests clarity drives this player's comfort more than action does.

**Sinks:** both naval sessions, and the long waits in the field and wall battles. The causes were a broken sea, an invisible fight state and no visible end condition.

## 5. Top issues, ranked by harm to a real player

### 1. Naval battles cannot be played

**What happened:** In both sea battles the ground looked like grass, rocky shore and stone road. Ships were drawn on land, and the map overview showed only a small pond. Orders never brought the fleet to the enemy, the camera kept leaving the ships, and no fight ever happened. In both naval sessions, the Enemy button showed "Their keep."

**Steps:** #78 to #96, #171 to #197.

**Screenshots:** shots/step-082.jpg, shots/step-095.jpg, shots/step-179.jpg, shots/step-184.jpg, shots/step-195.jpg

**Unclear point:** Some "orders" were taps on the minimap area. In information naval this was deliberate (#176, #181, #185, #189, #192, #194). In classical, the targets at x=700, y=120 to 150 sit where the minimap appears in other sessions (#83 at 679,78; #134 at 749,122). If a minimap tap only moves the camera, that alone would explain both the "drift" and the "ignored" orders. The grass rendering is still a real problem whatever the cause of the orders.

**Fix:**
- Make sure naval battles load on a tile with real sea.
- Render water clearly and spawn fleets on it.
- Make a minimap tap issue the order when attack-move is armed, or show clearly that the minimap only moves the camera.
- Replace "Their keep." with naval wording such as "Enemy fleet".

### 2. Land battles drag and have no visible goal or end

**What happened:** Stone field ran "many minutes at up to 4x" with "no clear victory goal". Bronze walls showed "little visible progress" at 3x. Gunpowder field was "slow even at 3x" and never finished. The "Base 1 vs 9/10" counter was the only score shown, and the player did not understand it.

**Steps:** #29 to #37, #58 to #74, #118 to #141, #163 to #166.

**Screenshots:** shots/step-037.jpg, shots/step-066.jpg, shots/step-113.jpg, shots/step-136.jpg

**Fix:**
- Show the victory condition on screen (for example "Destroy their HQ" or "Rout their army") with a progress bar for each side.
- Label the base counter.
- Add a timer or sudden-death rule for field battles.
- Make rout end fights sooner.

### 3. The fight is unreadable

**What happened:**
- Units are tiny and hidden under tree canopy, and pinch-out "did little".
- Health bars are thin lines, and melee becomes a "dense blob".
- Powers fire with no visible effect.
- In modern walls, the army went to "0 squads" with little warning.

**Steps:** #15 to #19, #49, #66, #113, #117, #152, #167 to #168.

**Screenshots:** shots/step-016.jpg, shots/step-019.jpg, shots/step-049.jpg, shots/step-113.jpg, shots/step-117.jpg, shots/step-152.jpg, shots/step-168.jpg

**Fix:**
- Fade or cut away trees over units.
- Add squad banners or icons with a strength bar, and switch to icons when zoomed out.
- Allow further zoom-out.
- Add a clear area effect and a floating label when a power fires.
- Add a "your army is losing" alert and a strength comparison bar.

### 4. Panels and menus cover the map or clip under the top bar

**What happened:**
- In bronze walls, the power menu opened at the top with Volley, Hold, Stop and Retreat cut off by the top bar.
- The path tooltip stayed over the bottom-left buttons for a long time.
- The info card, queue panel and pause menu all cover large parts of the map.

**Steps:** #11, #22, #27, #44, #48, #49, #51, #66, #115, #117, #132, #136.

**Screenshots:** shots/step-022.jpg, shots/step-027.jpg, shots/step-048.jpg, shots/step-049.jpg, shots/step-066.jpg, shots/step-117.jpg, shots/step-132.jpg, shots/step-136.jpg

**Fix:**
- Keep the power menu inside the safe area below the top bar.
- Make tooltips time out or close on the next tap.
- Dock info cards to one side at a smaller size.
- Make the pause panel compact.

### 5. The speed control is unreliable or unclear

**What happened:**
- In stone field the player tapped the top-bar speed button repeatedly and was unsure of the state (#32: "cycled back to 1x"; #35: "cycles to 2x").
- In bronze walls it "stayed at 1x" over five taps (#58 to #62).
- The pause panel picker worked (#64).

**Steps:** #12 to #14, #29 to #36, #58 to #65, #164 and #165.

**Screenshot:** shots/step-066.jpg

**Unclear point:** In stone this may be overshooting a cycling button while the battle runs in real time. In bronze the clipped power menu was overlapping the top bar at the time and may have been swallowing taps.

**Fix:**
- Show the current speed large on the button.
- Consider separate plus and minus buttons, or a small picker, instead of a cycle.
- Make sure no overlay can sit over the top bar.

### 6. Sack defenses end before the player plays

**What happened:** Kingdoms sack ended at 44 s, right after the player armed attack-move and before they picked a target (#101 to #102). Future sack ended at 1 min 15 s. The player called them "anticlimactic" and said they had "little tactical depth".

**Steps:** #99 to #102, #200 to #205.

**Screenshots:** shots/step-102.jpg, shots/step-205.jpg

**Fix:** Make raiders commit longer before withdrawing, or show their withdrawal threshold so the defense feels earned.

### 7. Camera shortcut buttons go to unexpected places

**What happened:**
- In stone field the Map button lit up but no map opened, even after two taps (#23, #24).
- In gunpowder field the player tapped what they thought was Enemy, and the camera went to their own HQ and opened its train panel (#131 to #132).
- In naval, Enemy and Find showed empty ground with a "Their keep" toast (#79, #80).

**Steps:** #23, #24, #79, #80, #131, #132, #178.

**Screenshots:** shots/step-132.jpg, shots/step-179.jpg

**Unclear point:** #131 tapped x=221, which in stone field (#20) was the "jump to base" button, while Enemy sat at x=171. This may be a missed tap or a layout that changes between sessions.

**Fix:** Keep the bottom-bar order fixed across battle types. Label buttons with text plus an icon. Make Enemy target the nearest enemy army, not a building.

### 8. Siege state and the defeat reason are not readable

**What happened:** After an attack-move order, the modern army "barely moved for over 40 seconds" at 1x (#147 to #152). Once the tanks reached the wall, the player could not tell whether a breach was coming. The defeat reason, "attackers' camp destroyed", confused the player, who was the attacker.

**Steps:** #147 to #152, #159 to #168.

**Screenshots:** shots/step-152.jpg, shots/step-168.jpg

**Fix:**
- Show wall and gate damage on the walls themselves.
- Mark the attackers' camp on screen as something to defend, with an alert when it is under attack.
- Show a moving-to-target line after an order so a slow march does not look like a freeze.

## 6. Bugs

There were no harness errors and no page errors.

**1. Naval sea drawn as land.**
- Open the sandbox and choose classical naval or information naval.
- Scroll down and tap Fight (#75, #76 or #171, #172).
- Tap Start in deploy (#77 or #173).
- Tap select all (#78 or #174).
- **Result:** the ships stand on grass or stone road, and the map overview shows a tiny pond (#82, #184).

**2. Naval fleet never reaches the enemy.**
- Follow the steps for bug 1.
- Arm attack-move (618,346).
- Tap toward the enemy at 700,120 (#85, #86, #89, #90) or on the minimap enemy dot at 750,123 (#181, #185).
- **Result:** the ships do not engage, the camera leaves the fleet, and no enemy is ever seen (#95, #195).
- **Caveat:** some of these taps were on the minimap and may only move the camera. Retest with taps on open water in the main view.

**3. Top-bar speed button does not change speed.**
- In bronze walls, tap Fight, then Start (#39, #40).
- Select all, then give orders until the power menu is open at the top (#41 to #44). How the menu opened is unclear: no tap on the powers button is logged in this session.
- Tap the speed button at 722,22 five times (#58 to #62).
- **Result:** the speed stays at 1x.
- **Workaround:** pause at 774,22, pick 3x, then Resume (#63 to #65).

**4. Power menu clipped under the top bar.**
- In bronze walls, with squads selected, open the power menu (state at #44).
- **Result:** Volley, Hold, Stop and Retreat are half hidden under the top bar and overlap the HUD (#48, #49, #66).
- In stone and gunpowder the same menu opened mid-screen without clipping (#18, #112), so this looks like it depends on the session or position.

**5. Map button does not open the map.**
- In stone field, during battle, tap Map at 271,346 twice (#23, #24).
- **Result:** the button highlights but no map opens.
- **Unclear point:** in classical naval a tap at 219,346 did open the overview (#81).

**6. Enemy button shows the wrong target or text.**
- In classical naval, tap Enemy at 170,346 (#79), then select all (#80).
- **Result:** the camera is on empty ground with a "Their keep" toast. The same happens in information naval at 170,341 (#178).
- In gunpowder field, a tap at 221,346 (#131) took the camera to the player's own HQ and opened its train panel. This may be a missed tap; see issue 7.

**7. Army stalls after an attack-move order (possible pathing issue).**
- In modern walls, tap Fight and Start (#143, #144).
- Select all, arm attack-move, then tap 560,290 (#145 to #147).
- **Result:** the army barely moves for 40 s at 1x (#148 to #152). A second order at the gate (#157) did work.

**8. Field battle cannot be finished (possible design gap).**
- In stone field, play with attack-move and speed at up to 4x (#7 to #36).
- **Result:** after many minutes the score still shows "Base 1 vs 9" and no end is reachable (#37). Gunpowder field behaved the same way (#141).

**9. Path tooltip lingers and steals a tap.**
- In gunpowder field, tap at 560,346 (#114).
- **Result:** a path tooltip opens instead of powers and covers the bottom-left buttons through #136.
- **Unclear point:** the powers button was at 467 to 486, so the first tap may simply have missed.

## 7. What was fun, and what else was good

**Best moments:**
- The result screens: Held, men lost on both sides, XP per regiment, houses ruined, general survived. They were clear at phone size every time (#102, #168, #205).
- The minimap jump, praised twice. It showed the enemy fort and the walled city with readable buildings and health bars (#135, #154).
- Seeing tanks reach the wall and getting a "Shaken" cue with a working Rally button. This was the most commander-like moment (#159 to #161).
- The power menu "has real choices" (#50): Charge, Volley, Hold, Stop, Line formation and Forced March all fired, and their counters dropped.

**Other good points:**
- Every battle started. There were no harness errors and no page errors.
- The setup to deploy to battle flow was reliable across all eight ages.
- The tutorial was short, three tips (#3 to #5).
- Training a laborer gave instant, clear feedback: queue at 10%, food down 50, and a confirmation message (#22).
- Bottom-bar buttons were "a good size" and "big enough to tap" (#96, #197).
- The pause panel speed picker worked reliably (#64, #125).
- Select all plus attack-move worked on land in every land session.
- Future sack ran with frustration at 1 for most of the battle, which shows a clean flow is already possible.

## 8. Coverage

**Played properly:**
- Battle setup, as a launch only.
- Select all and attack-move.
- Speed controls and pause.
- Powers, in stone, bronze, gunpowder and information naval.
- Result screens, three of them.

**Only glanced at:**

- **Buildings, sieges and walls:** viewed, but no breach, siege engine or wall interaction was seen.

**Missed:**
- Circle select, drawing a path, and selecting a single squad.

- Any naval combat.
- Building placement.

**Ages:** all eight were started. Only kingdoms sack, modern walls and future sack reached an outcome. Stone and bronze sessions ended without a "done" line, probably because their step budget ran out.
