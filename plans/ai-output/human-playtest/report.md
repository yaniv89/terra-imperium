# Human playtest: warmonger

# Playtest Report: Warmonger Persona, Campaign, Phone 844x390

## 1. Summary

A conquest-minded player took Akkad (later playing what looks like an Israel-based empire: Samaria, Jericho, Jerusalem) through four sessions: start, classical, gunpowder and modern, 218 steps in all. Setup, saving and the march order flow worked. The player's core goal, building an army and fighting battles personally, was repeatedly blocked:

- **Early game:** the war they declared had no reachable enemy.
- **Classical battle:** the first RTS battle gave them zero squads to command, and they lost.
- **Production on phone:** the production UI fought them every turn on this small screen.

Fun sat at 3 out of 5 for almost the whole run and never rose above it. Frustration started at 1 and settled at 2 to 3 from step 14 onward. No page errors were caught and every session opened on a working game screen.

**The three things that matter most:**

1. **Production UI on a phone is the biggest daily pain.**
   - The "Choose production" banner covers End Turn and keeps reopening city panels.
   - Unit rows and their add buttons sit under the bottom buttons.
   - In the late game the player pressed Advisor builds before almost every End Turn just to proceed (steps 148 to 200).
2. **The first player-commanded RTS battle was unplayable.** The player had 0 squads, their side showed 0%, and "Attack it" did nothing visible. It ended in defeat (steps 72 to 88).
3. **A warmonger gets no early fight.**
   - The game let the player pay 300 gold to declare war on a people they could not see or reach.
   - The Peoples screen contradicted itself about who had been met (steps 27 to 48).

## 3. Fun curve

**Steps 1 to 13: calm start.** Fun 3, frustration 1. Setup, tutorial and founding Kish all went smoothly. This is the lowest frustration of the run, but fun never rose above 3, so there was no real peak.

**Steps 14 to 20: first frustration rise.** Frustration went from 2 to 3. Choose production did nothing on two taps (14, 15) and the only military unit was tech-locked (18). Advisor builds was the escape (19).

**Steps 21 to 49: flat but hollow.** Frustration stayed at 2.
- War was declared at step 30, but no enemy could be found.
- The army marched and idled (41 to 45).
- The player called the early game "slow, mostly pressing End Turn and answering event cards" (48).

**Steps 50 to 71: anticipation.** The march on Sippar ("finally a real fight", 60) was offset by the cramped build list. Fun dropped to 2 for the first time (67 to 69) when the list would not scroll.

**Steps 72 to 90: the low point of the run.** Fun 2, frustration 3.
- The player chose Command to fight personally (72), which is exactly what this persona wants.
- In the battle they had 0 squads and their side showed 0% (82 to 84).
- A tap landed on Retreat instead of Switch to Auto (87), and the battle ended in defeat (88).
- Fun stayed at 2 until step 90.

**Steps 91 to 119: recovery.** Back to fun 3, frustration 2. Queueing Musketeers in several cities became a routine the player could perform.

**Steps 120 to 156: grinding friction.** Frustration was mostly 3.
- Hidden add buttons (120 to 125).
- A siege at 0% odds that had to be withdrawn (129 to 130).
- Hazor lost to raiders (147, fun 2).
- The production banner blocked End Turn and reopened panels (148 to 155).

**Steps 157 to 200: sawtooth.** Frustration swung 3 to 2 every two steps: press Advisor builds to clear the banner (3), then End Turn (2). This loop repeated about 15 times, and the player called it "repetitive" (188).

**Steps 201 to 218: modern siege.** The battle flow worked (208 to 212) and was the closest thing to a highlight. Frustration rose back to 3 as "my units are dwindling" (213 to 217). The session ended mid-battle with no result recorded.

## 4. Top issues (ranked by harm to a real player)

### 1. The "Choose production" banner blocks End Turn and keeps reopening city panels (phone)

**What happened:**
- From the gunpowder session onward, the player could not end the turn without first dealing with the production banner.
- Closing the city panel and popup did not help. The banner reopened things (148 to 154, 163 to 166).
- The workaround was pressing Advisor builds before nearly every End Turn: steps 167, 169, 173, 177, 180, 182, 185, 188, 193, 195, 197 and 199.
- This turned the late game into a two-tap chore and removed the player's own production choices.

**Evidence:**
- Steps 148 to 155 and 163 to 200.
- Session summaries at steps 156 and 218.
- Related screenshot: shots/step-122.jpg (banner covering the build list). No dedicated screenshot of the End Turn overlap was captured.

**Suggested fix:**
- Never let the production banner overlap End Turn on narrow screens. Stack it above, or merge it into the End Turn button as "Choose production (3 cities)".
- Let End Turn proceed with empty queues, perhaps with a one-time confirm.
- Offer a setting such as "auto-fill empty queues with advisor builds" so players stop being prompted every turn.

### 2. The build list is cramped, unscrollable at times, and hides add buttons

**What happened:**
- **Start session:** Choose production did nothing on two taps (14 to 15).
- **Classical session:** in Samaria, the list showed one row (Settlers) above the bottom buttons, and dragging did not scroll it (66 to 67). The player gave up and used Advisor builds (69).
- **Gunpowder session:** scrolling did work (97, 104, 112). However, in Jericho, tapping the Musketeers row twice did not queue it, and its add button was hidden behind the Advisor builds and Choose production banners (120 to 122). It took an extra scroll to expose the plus button (124 to 125).
- The player then repeated "scroll so the row clears the banner" for every city (133, 140).

**Evidence:**
- Steps 14 to 18, 63 to 69, 120 to 125, 133 and 140.
- Screenshots: shots/step-018.jpg, shots/step-067.jpg, shots/step-122.jpg.

**Unclear:**
- Whether tapping a row was ever meant to queue it. The plus button may be the only control by design.
- Why scrolling failed in classical but worked in gunpowder. It may depend on list length or the start point of the drag.

**Suggested fix:**
- Reserve bottom padding in the list equal to the height of the banner and buttons, so the last row can always scroll fully clear.
- Make the whole row tappable to queue, or at least open a detail view with a queue button.
- Check why Choose production is inert in the first city.

### 3. The first commanded RTS battle had 0 squads and could not be played

**What happened:**
- The player chose Command (72), began the defence of Shechem (74 to 75), deployed (76) and started (77).
- In battle, "All" showed 0 squads, their side showed 0%, and "Attack it" had no visible effect (82 to 84).
- They paused (85), tried to switch to Auto, but hit Retreat (86 to 87), and the army was routed (88).
- For a warmonger who wants to fight personally, this was the worst moment of the run.

**Evidence:**
- Steps 72 to 88.
- Screenshot: shots/step-084.jpg.

**Unclear:**
- Whether troops never deployed or died almost instantly.
- Whether the misplaced Retreat tap (87) caused the rout or the battle was already lost.
- Why the flow went from the Sippar attack prompt (72 to 73) into a "defend Shechem" prompt (74). The fate of the Sippar attack is not recorded.

The modern siege (204 to 217) did give the player commandable units, so the feature is not broken everywhere.

**Suggested fix:**
- If a battle starts with 0 deployable squads, block entry and explain why: no garrison, units elsewhere, or deployment failed.
- Show a clear "your forces: N squads" line on the deploy screen.
- Separate Retreat from Switch to Auto in the pause prompt, with more spacing and a confirm on Retreat.

### 4. War can be declared on an unmet, unreachable people, and nothing happens

**What happened:**
- On turn 5 the player paid 300 gold to declare war on Israel, whose army and land were not visible (28 to 31).
- By turn 7 they had a 9,700-strong army and nothing to fight (46).
- The Peoples header said "0 MET" while list entries showed MET tags (31).

**Evidence:**
- Steps 27 to 31, 46 and 48.
- Screenshots: shots/step-031.jpg, shots/step-046.jpg, shots/step-048.jpg.

**Suggested fix:**
- Disable or warn on war declarations against peoples with no known land or contact ("You have no way to reach them").
- Fix the MET count.
- Point the player to the nearest reachable rival or independent so an aggressive player gets a first target within the opening turns.

### 5. Raids overwhelm cities with no meaningful player response

**What happened:**
- Raids on Hazor were "badly outnumbered" twice. The player fought on auto and lost the city both times (146 to 147, 175 to 176).
- Fun dropped to 2 at step 147.

**Evidence:** Steps 146, 147, 175 and 176 (no notes or screenshots captured).

**Unclear:** The player had warning at least once ("raid party near Siwa in 3 turns", 171). They may simply not have reinforced, so this may be balance or player choice rather than a defect.

**Suggested fix:**
- Make raid warnings actionable, for example with a "send army" shortcut from the warning.
- Show garrison versus raider strength in the warning so the player can react before the battle prompt.

### 6. Siege and battle prompt layout on phone

**What happened:**
- In the Siege of Lachish panel, the Command and Auto buttons were cut off at the bottom, leaving only Withdraw clearly visible (129).
- In the modern session, the Command option did not register on the first two taps at x=285 and only worked at x=132 (201 to 203).

**Evidence:**
- Steps 129, 130 and 201 to 203.
- Screenshot: shots/step-129.jpg.

**Unclear:** Steps 201 to 202 may have been taps on a label rather than the button.

**Suggested fix:**
- Make the panel scrollable or compact at a height of 390.
- Enlarge the hit areas for the Command and Auto toggles to cover the full card.

### 7. Production times vary wildly with little explanation

**What happened:**
- The same unit (Musketeers) quoted 6, 7, 11, 28 and 150 turns in different cities (98, 100, 105, 113, 120).
- The player queued a 150-turn unit anyway, unsure whether it was worth it.

**Suggested fix:**
- Flag absurd build times ("this city has very low production; consider buying or building elsewhere").
- Offer a buy-with-gold option, since the player noted plenty of gold (184).

## 6. What was good

- **Setup:** fast and clear. From civ pick to playing took four taps with frustration 1 (1 to 4).
- **Save and load:** solid. Manual save and continue restored the exact turn 5 state (38, shots/step-038.jpg). Autosaves were noticed and welcomed (91, 179).
- **Marching:** the order flow is understandable (select army, March, pick tile, confirm), and the supply panel was called clear (46). The 3-turn ETA to Sippar built anticipation (60).
- **Event cards:** clear and fun to choose, with conquest-flavoured options like manpower, military horses, a punitive expedition and levees (21, 33, 115, 184, 218).
- **Modern siege flow:** the Enemy and "Attack it" buttons let the player hit base buildings in sequence, and rallying shaken pikemen worked (208 to 216). The player said "the RTS siege flow works" (218).
- **Odds display:** odds before a siege were readable, letting the player make an informed withdraw (129 to 130).
- **Turn speed:** turns pass quickly (94).
