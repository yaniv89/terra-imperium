# Human playtest: civ-veteran

# Playtest report: civ-veteran, campaign, phone 844x390 (en)

## 1. Summary

A veteran Civilization and Paradox player played 60 steps across six sessions: a fresh start, then saves in the bronze, classical, gunpowder, modern and information ages. The game was stable. No page errors were caught, and save, load, continue, the glossary and buying all worked cleanly. The player's own rating never moved off **fun 3/5** in any of the 60 steps, and frustration sat at 1 to 2.

The run spent its steps on menus and admin and never reached a decision with real stakes. No tech was picked, no diplomatic action was taken, no battle was fought, and no ship sailed. The flat fun line reflects that as much as any flaw in the game. The one big friction point was the tech tree, where the player spent ten steps looking for a single tech and never chose one.

**The three things that matter most:**
1. **The tech tree is hard to use on a phone.** It took six scrolls (steps 15 to 20) just to reach the individual techs, and an era tab tap did nothing visible (step 14). For this persona, the tech tree is a core screen.
2. **Closing the "Play full screen" tip also taps the map.** The same tap goes through and opens a tile card (steps 31 to 32, 41 to 43). It happens at the start of most sessions. The only clean workaround, Escape (step 51), does not exist on a phone.
3. **Late-game turns feel like busywork.** At turn 472, a stack of prompts plus "Choose production for 18 cities" (step 48) made turns feel heavy. An existing fix, "Advisor builds", queued 5 cities in one tap (step 59), but the player only found it one session later.

## 3. Fun curve

- **Fun was 3 at every one of the 60 steps.** There was no peak and no dip. For an impatient veteran, that suggests nothing delighted and nothing truly annoyed. It also fits the coverage: the player mostly opened menus and never saw a payoff such as a won battle, a finished tech or a signed treaty.
- **Frustration was the only line that moved:**
  - **Steps 1 to 14 (frustration 1):** setup, tutorial and the first event. These were smooth.
  - **Steps 15 to 20 (frustration 2):** the longest sink of the run, six steps in a row of hunting for individual techs. The player found "All techs" and opened Military, but never picked anything.
  - **Steps 22, 28, 30 (frustration 2):** short spikes.
    - Step 22: the tip X did not close on the first tap.
    - Step 28: the Trireme could not be bought and nothing said so.
    - Step 30: the Peoples tap only closed the city screen.
  - **Steps 31 to 32 and 42 to 43 (frustration 2):** the tip-close tap went through to the map, twice.
  - **Steps 33 to 41 (frustration 1):** the cleanest stretch, covering glossary, save, load and continue. This is the closest the run had to a high point.
  - **Steps 46, 48, 50 (frustration 2):**
    - Step 46: the Destroyer was out of reach (1320 against 1117 gold).
    - Steps 48 and 50: the late-game admin load.
  - **Steps 51 to 60 (mostly 1):** Escape closed the tip cleanly, buying worked, and Advisor builds cleared 5 cities in one tap. The single 2 at step 58 came just before the player found out Advisor builds worked.

## 4. Top issues (ranked by harm to a real player)

### 1. The tech tree takes too long to reach and pick a tech
- **What happened:**
  - The player opened Research (step 12) and tapped the Bronze tab, which did nothing visible (steps 14 to 15).
  - They then scrolled six times past other panels ("Fund scholars and focus", step 18) before finding "All techs" (step 19) and opening Military, which showed Hunting Bands (step 20).
  - The session ended there with no tech chosen.
  - The player brought it up again in steps 40 and 50 ("too many scrolls to find one tech").
- **Steps:** 12 to 20, plus 40 and 50.
- **Screenshots:** no dedicated shot. It is mentioned in shots/step-040.jpg.
- **Suggested fix:**
  - On phone, open Research on a short list of techs you can research now, each pickable in one tap.
  - Move scholar funding and focus below that list, or into a separate tab.
  - Make era tabs filter or jump the list visibly. If the Bronze tab did change something off-screen, scroll to it.

### 2. The tap that closes the "Play full screen" tip also hits the map
- **What happened:**
  - Tapping the X closed the tip, but the same tap opened a tile card underneath (step 32, and step 42 where the card read "Wild Land, Pontic Empire").
  - The player then had to spend a tap closing the card (steps 32 and 43).
  - In classical, the first X tap seemed not to close the tip at all (steps 21 to 22).
  - Escape closes it cleanly (step 51), but a phone player has no Escape key.
- **Steps:** 21 to 22, 31 to 32, 41 to 43.
- **Screenshots:** shots/step-042.jpg.
- **Suggested fix:**
  - Have the tip consume the pointer events that close it, using pointerdown and pointerup on the same layer.
  - Or ignore map taps for a short moment after the tip closes.
  - Also make the X hit area larger and confirm the first tap registers.

### 3. Late-game turns are buried in prompts
- **What happened:**
  - At turn 472 the player faced "Pick a landmark, 5 cities can build, Ruler points, +2 more" plus "Choose production for 18 cities" (step 48), and called it busywork.
  - In the information session they found "Advisor builds (5)", which queued all idle cities in one tap (steps 58 to 59), and praised it.
  - So the tool exists, but the player did not find it while it was most needed.
- **Steps:** 48, 58 to 59.
- **Screenshots:** shots/step-048.jpg.
- **Suggested fix:**
  - Put "Advisor builds" directly in the "N cities can build" prompt.
  - Add a "skip or auto the minor prompts" option, or a setting, for landmarks, ruler points and governors.

### 4. The Buy button does not show when you cannot afford it
- **What happened:**
  - The Trireme cost 634 to buy against 99 gold, but the button looked the same as an affordable one and gave no turns-to-afford (step 28).
  - The Destroyer at 1320 against 1117 gold was a similar case (step 46).
  - When affordable, buying was clear and had a confirm box (steps 55 to 57).
- **Steps:** 28, 46.
- **Screenshots:** shots/step-028.jpg.
- **Suggested fix:** Gray out unaffordable Buy buttons and show "affordable in N turns" based on current income (the step 28 shot shows +73 a turn).

### 5. Extra taps to reach screens
- **What happened:**
  - Opening a city took two taps both times it was tried: the first opened a tile card, the second the city (steps 25 to 26 and 52 to 53).
  - Tapping Peoples while the city screen was open only closed the city screen and left the tile card open, so a second tap was needed (steps 29 to 30).
  - These may be by design, or the first taps may have been slightly off. The diary does not make it clear.
- **Steps:** 25 to 26, 29 to 30, 52 to 53.
- **Screenshots:** none.
- **Suggested fix:**
  - Tapping your own city should open the city screen directly, or offer an obvious one-tap "Open city" on the card.
  - Top-level buttons such as Peoples should close any open panels and open in the same tap.

## 5. Bugs

**Bug A: the tip-close tap passes through to the map** (seen on two saves, the player reports three)
1. Load a gunpowder or modern save on phone, 844x390.
2. The "Play full screen" tip appears.
3. Tap its X at about (587, 72).
4. **Expected:** the tip closes and nothing else happens.
5. **Actual:** the tip closes and a tile card opens on the map underneath (steps 31 to 32 and 41 to 42, shots/step-042.jpg).

**Bug B (unclear): the tip X sometimes does not close on the first tap**
1. Load the classical save.
2. Tap the tip X at (586, 71) (step 21).
3. **Actual:** the player reports the tip did not close (step 22). A second tap at (587, 72) closed it (step 23).
4. This could be a missed tap. Worth checking the X hit area.

**Bug C (unclear): the Bronze era tab in Research does nothing visible**
1. In the bronze save, open Research (step 12).
2. Tap the Bronze tab at about (274, 286) (step 14).
3. **Actual:** "did nothing visible" (step 15). It may have changed content off-screen, or the tap may have missed.

**Bug D (unclear, possibly by design): the Peoples tap is swallowed by closing the city screen**
1. Open a city from its tile card (steps 25 to 27).
2. Tap Peoples at (770, 235) (step 29).
3. **Actual:** the city screen closes, the tile card stays open, and Peoples does not open (step 30).

**Not a game bug: the starting civ changed between sessions.** The player picked Ahhiyawa (step 1), but the bronze save loaded as Israel (step 12). This comes from the harness loading prepared saves. It did disrupt the player's naval plan built around Greece.

## 6. What was good

- **Setup and tutorial:** fast and clear. The Fast speed option suits impatient players (steps 1 to 10). The tip about the gold End Turn button was useful (step 9).
- **Glossary:** holding a readout opens its glossary entry, and the index has categories and search (steps 33 to 35). This is a strong help system for a veteran.
- **Save and load:**
  - Save gave instant confirmation ("Saved, turn 331").
  - The load slots were clear.
  - Continue returned to the same spot in under a second, with prompts kept (steps 37 to 40, shots/step-040.jpg).
- **Map controls:** pinch-zoom and pan were smooth (steps 23 to 24).
- **Army card and March mode:** clear on supply, fortify bonus, March/Hold and the fleet in port. The March hint bar ("tap a city or a tile to march to") includes a Path option (steps 47 to 50).
- **Buying:** a confirm box shows cost and finish time, and gold updates correctly (1984 to 392) (steps 55 to 57).
- **Advisor builds:** queued 5 idle cities in one tap (step 59). This is exactly the late-game relief this persona asks for.
- **End Turn feedback:** "The world moves..." progress bar (step 60).
- **Stability:** no page errors in any session.
