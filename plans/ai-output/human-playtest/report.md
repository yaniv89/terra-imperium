# Human playtest: warmonger

# Playtest Report: Warmonger Persona, Campaign (phone 844x390, English)

## 1. Summary

A warmonger persona played 329 steps across three sessions: a fresh start as Akkad (turns 1 to 13), a classical-era save as Israel at war with Akkad (turns about 118 to 126), and a gunpowder-era save as Israel at war with Saba (turns 331 to 348). The core strategy layer is in good shape. Setup is smooth, save and load work, and buying with gold, event cards, pacts and attack previews are clear. The war loop, which is the reason this persona plays, keeps breaking down at the end of each fight. The player fought three battles itself and lost the whole army each time, usually without seeing the clash. Auto battles often returned to the map with no result screen, and in one case a city fell without the player being told. The player never won a battle in 329 steps. Fun sat at 3 almost the whole time, reached 4 only around declaring war and starting battles, and fell to 2 after each battle loss.

**The three things that matter most:**
1. **Battle outcomes are often invisible.** Auto defences and "Switch to Auto" drop the player on the map with no result. Lachish fell and the player only found out from a truce prompt (steps 218, 273, 294, 298, 300).
2. **Commanded battles lose whole armies fast and out of sight**, and "Finish now" does not end the battle (steps 81 to 91, 243 to 251, 292 to 293). A warmonger who follows the game's own prompts loses everything and learns nothing.
3. **Odds and feedback are inconsistent or silent in key war moments.** Auto said 100% hold against 77,600 attackers and later 0% against a similar threat (271, 296). The result screen showed "Yours 16,160 of 16,160" after a total loss (92). Demand tribute and the white peace offer gave no response (33 to 35, 281 to 282).

## 3. Fun curve

- **Steps 1 to 64 (start session): flat fun 3, frustration 1.** Setup, founding Kish, buying a Warband and marching to the border were smooth but not exciting. A small rise in frustration came at 26 to 27 (the tech tree loop, fun 2 frustration 3) and at 34 to 35 (silent tribute, fun 2 frustration 3).
- **Steps 65 to 86: first peak, fun 4.** Declaring war on Israel (65), marching on Jerusalem and starting a commanded siege (71 to 79) was the best stretch of the start session. Frustration crept up to 2 when the squads went idle at 81.
- **Steps 87 to 95: first sink, fun 2, frustration 3.** The army was wiped, "Finish now" did not end the battle (88), the player had to go through the pause menu (89 to 91), and the result screen misread the losses (92). The only army was gone on turn 8 or so.
- **Steps 96 to 110:** a slow recovery to fun 3, with frustration stuck at 2 for the rest of the session.
- **Steps 111 to 219 (classical): mostly fun 3, frustration 1 to 2.** Small peaks of fun 4 came with the sea landing at Latakia (124 to 126), picking the War era goal (147), the Akkad siege alert (203) and the 100% Auto defence (216). The low point was 181 (outpost surprise, fun 2). Frustration was 2 through most of the map hunting at 157 to 161 and 208 to 214.
- **Steps 234 to 247 (gunpowder): second peak, fun 4.** Attacking Zafar and commanding the siege.
- **Steps 248 to 251: second sink, fun 2, frustration 3.** The pikemen were destroyed off screen in about 30 seconds.
- **Steps 288 to 291: third peak, fun 4.** Commanding the Lachish defence, which then collapsed at 292 to 294 (frustration 3, no result screen).
- **Steps 296 to 300: frustration 3.** Contradictory odds, then the discovery that Lachish had fallen silently.
- **Steps 301 to 329: flat fun 3, frustration 2.** Truce, events and a repeated advisor plus end turn loop. Events (303, 312, 316, 326) gave some texture, but there was no excitement.

**Pattern:** fun goes up only when a battle starts and drops every time one ends. The peaks are built on anticipation, and the payoff never arrives.

## 4. Top issues (ranked by harm to a real player)

### 1. Battles end with no result, including losing a city
- **What happened:** Auto defences at 218, 273 and 298 closed with no result screen. "Switch to Auto" after the army was wiped at 293 also dropped straight to the map (294). At 300 the player learned from a truce prompt that Lachish had fallen. The Hazor raid at 309 did show a result, so the screen exists but is skipped in some paths.
- **Steps:** 217 to 218, 272 to 273, 293 to 294, 297 to 298, 300, 309.
- **Screenshots:** shots/step-294.jpg
- **Fix:** Always show a result card after any battle, whatever the path (Auto from the start, Switch to Auto mid-battle, Finish now). Include who won, losses, wall damage and whether the city changed hands. Add a turn report line such as "Lachish has fallen to Saba".

### 2. Commanded battles wipe the army off screen with no readable fight
- **What happened:** In both commanded sieges the player selected all, pressed Attack, tapped the enemy buildings, and the army went idle (81) or ran off screen (243). The units were then destroyed within seconds. At Zafar the only sign was a "Pikemen destroyed" toast (248). "Find" did not show the units in time (243, 247). The defence at Lachish went from attack order to 3% in one step (290 to 292).
- **Steps:** 77 to 87, 239 to 249, 289 to 293.
- **Screenshots:** shots/step-248.jpg, shots/step-092.jpg
- **Unclear:** At 79 the squads may have gone idle because the tap missed the buildings. The pre-battle odds also warned "Unlikely" (71) and "bad odds" (235), so the losses may be correct balance. The problem is mainly how readable the loss is.
- **Fix:** When the player's units come under attack, pan the camera to them or show a minimap ping. Make "Find" follow the selected group. Consider having attack-moves stop at visible threats. Show a short combat log ("Pikemen engaged by 3 musketeer squads").

### 3. "Finish now" does not end the battle, and the result screen misreports losses
- **What happened:** At 0% army, "Finish now" closed the dialog but the battle kept running (88) or seemed to fast-forward (250). In the first battle the player had to open the pause menu and scroll to find Continue (89 to 91). The result read "Yours 16,160 of 16,160" with a full bar after a total loss (92).
- **Steps:** 87 to 92, 249 to 251.
- **Screenshots:** shots/step-092.jpg
- **Fix:** When the player's army is wiped, "Finish now" should go straight to the result screen. Label the result rows clearly as start strength vs. survivors, and empty the bar to match losses.

### 4. Auto defence odds look contradictory
- **What happened:** Auto said "100% likely hold" for 4,900 militia against 77,600 (271). Two turns later it said 0% for a similar siege (296). At 216 it also said 100%. Lachish then fell.
- **Steps:** 216, 271, 296, 300.
- **Unclear:** Walls (40% at 287) or a siege-turn counter may explain the swing, but nothing on screen said so.
- **Fix:** Show the reasons behind the odds on the defence card ("Walls hold this turn; breach expected in 2 turns"). A 100% that turns into a lost city damages trust in the number.

### 5. A warmonger can lose the game's whole army on turn 8 with little warning and a slow recovery
- **What happened:** The only Warband was bought, marched and lost in the first war (20 to 91). Rebuilding was slow because Legion and other armies were locked behind techs (99). The summary at 110 calls the start "hard and punishing". The same thing happened in gunpowder (all 10k lost at 251, then a truce at 300).
- **Steps:** 20 to 110, 251 to 300.
- **Fix:** Make the "Unlikely" or "Hopeless" warning stronger before a commanded attack, for example by requiring a confirm when the odds are low. Give a defeated army a chance to retreat or survive in part. Signpost the first army techs in the build list ("unlocks with Hunting Bands, 3 turns").

### 6. March targeting and the camera fight the player
- **What happened:** Tapping Sippar as a march target made the camera jump to Anatolia (159). Targets sit behind the march banner (157, 276 to 277). Finding Shechem off screen took five steps, and the search-looking button opened zoom tools (208 to 212). The player hit Siwa (41k defenders) by accident (212 to 213).
- **Steps:** 157 to 160, 208 to 213, 276 to 277.
- **Screenshots:** shots/step-159.jpg
- **Fix:** Keep the camera on the chosen target. Move the march banner away from the target or make it collapsible. Add a city search or a list of your own and enemy cities in march mode. If the magnifier icon is not search, change it.

### 7. Diplomatic actions fail or get no answer
- **What happened:** "Demand tribute" (showing "Would yield, 1 decree") did nothing when tapped twice (33 to 35). The card looked grey with no reason, possibly because decrees were at 0 of 3. The white peace offer went out with no visible yes or no (281 to 282).
- **Steps:** 33 to 35, 280 to 282.
- **Screenshots:** shots/step-035.jpg
- **Fix:** Disabled actions should say why ("Needs 1 decree, you have 0"). Every offer should get a visible reply.

### 8. The late game turns into a chore loop
- **What happened:** Steps 310 to 328 were almost all "advisor fills queue, end turn". The player noted that the prompt stack in the top left feels noisy (329).
- **Fix:** Add an option to auto-refill queues with the advisor, and batch "needs production" prompts. During truces, give the player a military goal to work toward.

### 9. Outpost stage not signposted when founding
- **What happened:** "Best site" put a city on the coast for ships, but it became an outpost needing 29 turns before it could build anything (152 to 153, 181).
- **Screenshots:** shots/step-181.jpg
- **Fix:** Show "Starts as an outpost, about 29 turns to build" in the site picker.

### 10. Naval transport is unclear
- **What happened:** Manual boarding and landing worked (115 to 124). Later, the player marched an army "across the water" next to a trireme, and the game plotted a 4-turn route (200), but it never became clear whether the army boarded (219, item 4). Open ocean was locked with no clear reason (118).
- **Fix:** Show "Will embark on Trireme" (or "Needs a ship") in the march preview, and explain the ocean lock in the tooltip.

### Minor
- Autosave toast covers the "Ruler points to spend" prompt (108, shots/step-108.jpg).
- The "Defend" button on a news line opened the city panel instead of sending troops (204). It was a step away from "Send an army" (206), so this may be by design, but the label sets a different expectation.
- Ruler attribute Military was below the fold (257 to 258).
- Era goals ended at 0 of their goals met and were never understood (110).
- March was greyed after landing with no reason given (127).
- The tip X on loaded saves needed two taps (111 to 112). It may be a missed tap.

## 5. Bugs

1. **"Finish now" does not end a lost battle**
   - Start or load a game and declare war on a neighbour (62 to 65).
   - Attack a city and choose Command, then Begin Battle (70 to 73).
   - Let your army be wiped, then tap "Finish now" (87).
   - Expected: the result screen. Actual: the dialog closes and the battle keeps running or fast-forwards (88, 250). In the first case the result was only reached through the pause menu (89 to 91).

2. **Result screen shows full strength after a total loss**
   - Same as bug 1, then reach the result screen (91).
   - The screen showed "Yours 16,160 of 16,160" with a full bar (92, shots/step-092.jpg).

3. **No result screen after Auto defence or Switch to Auto**
   - Wait for the AI to besiege your city (216, 271, 296).
   - Choose Auto and tap Fight on Auto (217, 272, 297), or command and then tap "Switch to Auto" after your army dies (293).
   - Expected: an outcome card. Actual: straight back to the map (218, 273, 294, 298). Compare the raid at 308 to 309, where a result screen did appear.

4. **City lost with no notification**
   - Same as bug 3, gunpowder save, Lachish sieged at 296 with Auto at 0%.
   - Fight on Auto (297), then end the turn (299).
   - The player only learned Lachish fell from the truce offer at 300.

5. **Camera jumps away from the march target**
   - Select an army, tap March (156), and tap an enemy city (Sippar, 158).
   - The camera moved to Anatolia (Sinope, Amaseia), and the target was out of view (159, shots/step-159.jpg).

6. **Demand tribute gives no feedback** (possibly an intended disabled state with no explanation)
   - Open Peoples, then Kingdom of Israel (32).
   - Tap "Demand tribute" (33, 34).
   - Nothing happened: no message and no reason shown (35, shots/step-035.jpg).

7. **White peace offer gets no reply** (possibly a missed confirmation)
   - At war, tap the AT WAR chip, then the peace option (279 to 280).
   - Offer white peace (281).
   - No yes or no was seen (282).

8. **Tech tree "all techs" seems to loop** (unclear, could be tap placement)
   - Open research from the map (23).
   - Alternate between the full tree button at about (681, 281) and the tech chooser at about (619, 346) (24 to 26).
   - The player kept returning to the same panel (27).

9. **Auto odds may be wrong** (unclear, could be walls or a timing effect)
   - In the gunpowder save, a 77,600 siege on Lachish against 4,900 militia showed "100% likely hold" (271).
   - The next siege showed 0% (296), and the city fell (300).

## 6. What was good

- **Setup is fast and clear.** Civ, map, rules and ready took 9 taps (1 to 9).
- **Save, load and autosave work**, and orders survive a reload (44 to 47, 215, 319).
- **The war declaration** shows its cost (300 gold, opinion hit) before you confirm (64 to 65).
- **Attack previews are honest and useful.** "Hopeless, 21,600 against 10,000, 0% on Auto, out of supply" stopped bad marches (159, 278). The player praised them twice.
- **Buying with gold** always shows a confirm box with the price, and gold changes exactly as promised (168, 187, 267, 304, 317, 327).
- **Event cards** give choices that feel meaningful to the persona: manpower, a border fort, swearing revenge, feeding a city, buying manpower, repairing gates (22, 105, 312, 316, 326).
- **Pacts** are easy to sign and the pact count updates (229 to 230, 306 to 307).
- **The sea landing** at Latakia worked from start to finish and was a fun high point (115 to 124).
- **Hold and Idle** work as expected (175 to 176), and the supply warning sent the army home in time (171).
- **The advisor build button** makes 15 cities manageable (329).
- **Battle controls respond**: deploy, Line formation, select all, Attack, the Enemy jump and Rally Cry (73, 237, 239 to 245, 86, 292). The battle intro cards (hills, walls, supply) were clear (72, 287).
- **No page errors** were caught across 329 steps.
