# Human playtest: civ-veteran

# Playtest Report: civ-veteran, campaign, phone 844x390

## 1. Summary

A Civ and Paradox veteran played 397 steps across eight saves, from the opening turns through the future age. All eight sessions opened and were played; the harness logged no page errors. The strategic layer is solid. Setup is quick, save and load work, event cards read well, Advisor builds is a real time saver, and the peace screen was praised as better than Civ's and close to EU4's.

The real-time battles drove almost every low point. In four separate fights the player's only army dropped to 0 with no message, then the player sat watching an empty field until the clock ran out. Strategic war feedback had similar gaps:

- Pre-battle odds left out auxiliaries.
- "March to" shortcuts walked the army into garrisons 20 to 30 times its size without warning.
- A peace was forced mid-counterattack with no heads-up.
- An amphibious assault the player never ordered cost 6,720 men.

The late game (information and future sessions) turned into an Advisor builds, then End Turn loop with no pressure and no real choices.

**The three things that matter most:**
1. **Silent unit loss in RTS battles.** After it happens the player has no agency: they cannot recruit, cannot see what happened, and can only wait (steps 87, 132, 175, 224).
2. **Hidden or missing information before war actions.** This covers auxiliaries left off odds cards, hopeless sieges reached via shortcuts, forced peace without warning, and an unordered assault (steps 100, 197, 231 to 237, 286).
3. **The late game is a chore.** It is an Advisor builds, then End Turn loop with a gold glut. The "free law change" and "Appoint governors" chips lead nowhere (steps 279, 289, 299 to 347, 370).

## 3. Fun curve

- **Start (1 to 50):** Fun stayed at 3 and frustration at 1. The only bump was the double virtue confirm at step 21 (frustration 2). Calm and competent, but never exciting.
- **Bronze (51 to 100):**
  - **First peak.** The Siege of Jerusalem lifted fun to 4 at steps 75, 79, 81, 83 and 85. This was the first real stakes in the run.
  - **Hidden buttons.** Frustration hit 3 at steps 76 to 80 because the defence buttons were under toasts.
  - **Sharp drop.** At step 87 the army went from 52% to 0% with no message. Fun fell to 2 and frustration rose to 3 for steps 87 to 94 while the player waited on an empty field.
  - **Session end.** It closed at frustration 3 (step 100) after the forced peace cancelled the counterattack.
- **Classical (101 to 149):** Flat at 3 and 1 or 2, until the warband vanished after Rally Cry (132). Fun fell to 2 and frustration rose to 3 for steps 132 to 137, then recovered to 3 and 2 with a "hollow win" (139).
- **Kingdoms (150 to 198):**
  - **Weak start.** The unwinnable raid set fun to 2 at steps 150 to 153.
  - **Second peak.** The Polemonium siege set fun to 4 at steps 161 to 163 and 166.
  - **Worst moment so far.** Step 175, frustration 4, when the only warband vanished off-screen with no feedback. Fun stayed at 2 through step 180, again at 185 (locked builds), and at 197 (the unordered assault).
- **Gunpowder (199 to 248):**
  - **Brief high.** The peace screen at step 203 (fun 4, frustration 1).
  - **Frustration peaks.** Frustration reached 4 at steps 226 to 228, with no barracks, a burning HQ and no agency. It reached 4 again at step 233, the 4:1 Tirzah loss.
- **Modern (249 to 298):** Mostly 3 and 2. Dips to fun 2 and frustration 3 came at:
  - steps 265 to 267 (locked builds)
  - steps 274 to 279 (law chip dead end)
  - steps 286 and 287 (the 228k garrison)
  - steps 296 and 297 (the raid again)

  One fun 4 at step 285, the hope of a battle.
- **Information (299 to 348):** Fun 3 and frustration 2 at every step. Nothing went wrong, and nothing was fun. This is the clearest sign of the late-game chore problem.
- **Future (349 to 397):** Again flat at 3 and 2. Frustration rose to 3 during the second law chip hunt (367 to 374) and the Idle badge confusion (385). The single fun 4 was the Mars base event (388).

**Overall:** Fun never reached 5 and frustration never reached 5. Every peak (fun 4) came from war stakes, either a siege or a battle starting. Every frustration 4 came from RTS battles or the Tirzah auto-resolve.

## 4. Top issues (ranked by harm to a real player)

**1. Armies vanish in RTS battles with no alert, and the player is left with nothing to do**
- **What happened:** Four times the player's force dropped to 0 within seconds:
  - Jerusalem, after Rally at step 87
  - Sippar defence, after Rally Cry at step 132
  - Polemonium, off-screen in fog at step 175
  - Zafar, at 3x speed at step 224

  There was no death or rout message and no camera jump. Housing and base counts changed silently at the same time. Twice the player suspected Rally caused it. Afterwards the player could only wait on an empty field (88 to 92, 134 to 138, 177 and 178). The headquarters trains only laborers, and a nearby building couldn't be selected (225, 226).
- **Screenshots:** shots/step-087.jpg, shots/step-132.jpg, shots/step-175.jpg, shots/step-224.jpg, shots/step-226.jpg
- **Fix:**
  - Show a "squad routed" or "squad destroyed" toast with a camera jump.
  - Clarify what Rally does and whether it fired.
  - Explain what "You N%" measures.
  - When no army remains, offer "Concede" or "Auto-finish" instead of forcing a wait.
  - Give a clear hint for where soldiers are trained.

**2. War decisions are made without the information that decides them**
- **What happened:**
  - **Tirzah.** The odds card said "Auto holds 0%" while the player had 14,900 men against 10,000 (231). The player lost 14,850 to 3,500 (233). Only the full report revealed 21,820 vs 31,640 auxiliaries (237).
  - **Zafar.** "March to Zafar" took the army straight to a 228k to 342k garrison against 10k men, with no warning (286).
- **Screenshots:** shots/step-231.jpg, shots/step-233.jpg, shots/step-237.jpg, shots/step-286.jpg
- **Fix:**
  - Show expected auxiliaries and the full force estimate on the pre-battle card.
  - Have March-to shortcuts warn about the odds before committing.
  - If a garrison is really 20 to 30 times the army for a less advanced civ, check the garrison scaling.

**3. Strategic events happen to the player, not with them**
- **What happened:**
  - Peace was forced on turn 87 while a 20k army was marching to retake Jerusalem, with no warning that war weariness was near the limit (100).
  - The Sippar siege marker disappeared without a message (148).
  - A turn report announced a repelled amphibious assault on Sinope costing 6,720 men that the player never ordered (197). It is unclear whether this was an automated army or a stale order.
- **Screenshots:** shots/step-197.jpg (no screenshot for steps 100 or 148)
- **Fix:**
  - Warn a turn ahead when forced peace is coming, or offer a peace prompt.
  - Report when sieges end, and why.
  - Name the army and who ordered any attack, and ask before auto-launching one.

**4. Siege defence buttons are hidden under toasts**
- **What happened:** On the defence card, Withdraw, Auto, Command and the confirm button sit under the "Army idle" toast and the "Defend Jerusalem" banner (76). Taps on the visible edge failed (75, 77). The player only got through by discovering the card scrolls (78, 79). The same overlap recurred at steps 80 and 122. This hits every city defence.
- **Screenshot:** shots/step-076.jpg
- **Fix:** Suppress or move toasts while a decision card is open, and anchor the card's buttons above the bottom toast area.

**5. The late game is a repetitive tap loop with no pressure**
- **What happened:** Steps 299 to 347 and 381 to 396 are almost entirely Advisor builds, then End Turn, with flat fun 3 and frustration 2. At the same time:
  - Gold reached 25k to 33k at about +1,900 per turn with nothing to spend it on (338, 348).
  - Events offered gold against slightly more gold (326).
  - The Kerma war moved on without the player and nothing pointed back to it (348).
- **Screenshots:** none attached (summary at step 348)
- **Fix:**
  - Add a per-city "auto-produce" toggle so cities stop asking.
  - Add late-game gold sinks.
  - Give event cards real trade-offs.
  - Surface active wars on the turn report, with a nudge to act.

**6. The "free law change" and "Appoint governors" chips lead to a generic screen**
- **What happened:** The law chip opened the Empire overview. The player scrolled through Court, Stability and Advisors and then the Ruler tab, and found no laws, in two separate sessions (269 to 279, 364 to 374). The governors chip did the same (288, 289). By contrast, the "Pick a landmark" chip went straight to the right place (305, 306), which shows the pattern can work.
- **Screenshots:** shots/step-279.jpg, shots/step-370.jpg
- **Fix:** Deep-link each chip to its picker. If laws live somewhere unexpected, the chip should scroll to and highlight that section.

**7. The recurring raid on Hazor can't be countered**
- **What happened:** Hazor was sacked four times, every 24 turns (T111, T135, T159, T183), each time 2,000 militia against 20,000 raiders (152). It happened again in 1938 CE with 15,760 warbands (296). A 3-turn warning existed in the modern session, but nothing showed how to reinforce in time. The report also said both "none of yours lost" and "2,200 lost" (152).
- **Screenshots:** shots/step-152.jpg, shots/step-296.jpg
- **Fix:**
  - Scale town defence with the era.
  - Add counterplay (walls, tribute, a "send army" button on the warning).
  - Fix the contradictory loss text.

**8. Build list problems: locked items look buildable, and the rush button failed**
- **What happened:**
  - **Locked items.** Pikemen legion and Siege train showed turn counts and a + button but were locked behind a tech (185). In the modern age almost every entry was locked, so the player gave up and used the advisor (265 to 267). A + button was half under the bottom bar (264).
  - **Rush button.** Rush 866 gold with 1,551 gold available did nothing on two taps, with no error (243, 244).
- **Screenshots:** shots/step-185.jpg, shots/step-267.jpg, shots/step-244.jpg
- **Fix:**
  - Grey out and sort locked items to the bottom, or add a "buildable only" filter.
  - Make rush either work or show as disabled with a reason.

**9. The Idle badge never clears because of stranded settlers**
- **What happened:** The Idle 3 badge stayed after Advisor builds (383, 384) because it counted settlers with "No good site is known in reach" on a fully settled map (385). Earlier, the player cancelled settle mode on the same settlers (291).
- **Screenshot:** shots/step-385.jpg
- **Fix:**
  - Split the badge into cities and units.
  - Let stranded settlers join a city or disband.

**10. RTS order clarity**
- **What happened:**
  - "Attack it" with nothing selected silently did nothing (166 to 170, 216 and 217).
  - The siege goal grew from 2 to 3, then 5, then 6 base buildings with no explanation (171, 175).
- **Screenshot:** shots/step-171.jpg
- **Fix:**
  - Make "Attack it" auto-select all units, or say "nothing selected".
  - Explain why the target count changes, for example new buildings constructed.

## 6. What was good

- **Peace and deal screen.** Praised twice: "better than the Civ deal screen" (157) and "close to EU4's" (203). It shows score costs, how far short the player is, and why the AI refuses (capital secure -10, odds -4). White peace with Pontus went through cleanly with a visible truce end (324, 325).
- **Advisor builds.** Fills every idle city in one tap (193, 300). It kept a 397-step run moving.
- **Setup and onboarding.** World setup takes five taps (1 to 5), and the tutorial is four short cards (6 to 9).
- **Save and load.** Save shows feedback, the load list is clear, and the autosave reload worked (27 to 30). Turn reports include autosave notes (181, 290).
- **March preview.** Shows turns, supply cost and attack framing (48, 98, 105).
- **Best site for settlers.** Found a valid coastal spot when everything nearby was red (117, 118).
- **Event cards.** Readable costs and some historical flavour ("Nice nod to the history", 64). Clear choices across eras (230, 338, 388).
- **World Congress.** Voting was easy and the tally was visible (378 to 380).
- **Battle result screens and full reports.** Clear even in defeat (93, 179), and the full report explained the auxiliaries (237).
- **"Pick a landmark" chip.** Went straight to the right screen (306), a model for the other chips.
- **Battle loading and 3x speed.** Both work reliably (82, 88, 134).
