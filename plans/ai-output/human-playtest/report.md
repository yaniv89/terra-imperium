# Human playtest: civ-veteran

# Playtest Report: civ-veteran, campaign, phone 844x390

## 1. Summary

A Civilization and Paradox veteran played 418 steps across six saves, from the stone-age start to the information age. No page errors were caught and every session opened on a working game screen. The strategic layer holds up well:

- Setup, event cards, era goals, the World Congress, governors, laws, the glossary, save and load, and buying with a confirm step were all clear and quick.
- Turns pass fast and autosave is reliable.

The game falls down at the points that should be its showpiece:

- **Battles.** Every manually commanded battle (steps 92-106, 225-242, 298-317) ended in a total wipe-out. The battle forecasts contradicted each other badly. One post-battle report said Auto would have won 100% of the time.
- **Naval.** Naval play was effectively unreachable.
- **Late game.** The late game was mostly pressing End Turn.

Fun sat at 3 out of 5 almost the whole way, never went above 4, and frustration reached 4 three times. All three were in battle or war forecasts.

**The three things that matter most:**

1. **Battle forecasts are not trustworthy.** Zafar was shown as 12k-18k "Likely win", then 44.9k in the battle report, then 294.9k "Hopeless", all within two turns. Jerusalem flipped from "holds 100%" to "0% Hopeless" against a smaller army. Players cannot make war decisions on these numbers.
2. **Manual RTS command is a trap.** "Attack it" sent every unit to its death. Retreat seemed to destroy the army. "Finish now" did not end the battle. The report then said Auto always wins. The headline feature punishes the player for using it.
3. **Silent failures and hidden rules.** Diplomacy buttons that cannot work do nothing and say nothing. Ships are hard to find. New cities are dead Outposts for 29 turns with no warning. There is no full tech tree view. A veteran expects to be told why something is blocked.

## 3. Fun curve

**Start (1-70).** Fun held at 3 with frustration at 1, through setup, research, founding, events and save/load. Frustration first rose to 2 when the era tabs did nothing (13, 50-54) and the pact failed silently (23). It reached 3 when the player gave up looking for the full tech tree (55). Fun dropped to 2 at steps 65-66, when war was blocked by a 300-gold cost with no feedback and the player felt the early game would never reach battles or ships.

**Bronze (71-140).** Fun hit 4 for the first time when a 25k Pontic army marched on Jerusalem (80). It stayed at 4 through the siege briefing, deploy and tips (92-101), which was the best stretch of the run. It then crashed to fun 2, frustration 4, at step 102, when one "Attack it" order wiped the whole army in about 40 seconds. Three more dips followed:
- Fun 2 at 117, when no city could build a ship.
- Fun 2 at 127, when rebuilding the army would take 24 turns.
- Fun 2 at 132-133, when the siege odds flipped from 100% to 0%.

**Classical (141-210).** This was the steadiest and most enjoyable session. Fun reached 4 at:
- Step 173, with Best site.
- Steps 191-193, with the threat alert and Send an army.
- Steps 202-204, with a clutch gold buy of archers before Akkad arrived.
- Step 207, defending Shechem.

The only dip was at 187, the 29-turn Outpost (fun 2, frustration 3).

**Gunpowder (211-279).** The commanded battle at Zafar ran at fun 4 (224-236). It ended in a loss at 237-242, but the clear report kept fun at 3. Fun dipped to 2 at 264, when nothing was affordable. It rose back to 4 at 268-273 after a +2000 gold windfall bought a Frigate, and stayed at 4 for the era goal (278).

**Modern (280-348).** Fun was 4 again for the second commanded siege (296-307). It sank to 2 for the whole stretch from 308 to 317: the forecast was wrong, Retreat failed, the battle would not end, and the report said Auto would have won. Frustration peaked at 4 at steps 316 and 331, the latter when the third conflicting strength figure for Zafar appeared.

**Information (349-418).** Fun was flat at 3 and frustration at 1-2. The player made peace on both fronts, picked six backlogged landmarks in a row, and then ended turns from 393 to 416 with only event cards in between. The player flagged this as boring at 417.

**Overall.** Peaks always came from looming wars and the start of battles. Troughs always came from how those battles resolved.

## 4. Top issues (ranked by harm to a real player)

### 1. Battle and siege forecasts contradict each other

**What happened:**
- Jerusalem with 3,000 militia was shown as "Auto holds 100%" against 33,340 attackers. The next turn it showed "0%, Hopeless" against 30,220.
- Zafar went from "Likely win" with 11,920-17,880 defenders, to 44,900 in the battle report, to 294,900 "Hopeless" two turns later.

**Steps:** 130, 132, 297, 314, 331

**Screenshots:** shots/step-130.jpg, shots/step-132.jpg, shots/step-297.jpg, shots/step-314.jpg, shots/step-331.jpg

**Suggested fix:**
- Use one source of truth for enemy strength.
- Show the modifiers that drive the verdict, such as walls, unit quality and relief forces. The briefing at 299 mentioned 30k relief that the forecast did not include.
- If scouting is uncertain, show the range and its confidence rather than a confident "Likely win".

### 2. Manual battle command leads to total wipe-outs, and the controls misbehave

**What happened:**
- "Attack it" sent every unit off with no warning, and the army was gone in about 40 seconds (102). The stale camp popup then covered the defeat dialog (103).
- In the modern siege the army collapsed from 100% to 17% within seconds at 3x (308).
- Retreat produced "Your army is gone" while the selected squad chip still showed 97% (311).
- "Finish now" closed the prompt but the battle kept running. Only the pause icon ended it (237-238, 312-314).
- The final report said "Auto wins this battle 100% of the time" right after a total manual loss (316).
- After switching to Auto mid-battle, the player was returned to the map with no result screen (106).

**Steps:** 101-106, 236-242, 305-317

**Screenshots:** shots/step-102.jpg, shots/step-308.jpg, shots/step-311.jpg, shots/step-314.jpg, shots/step-316.jpg

**Suggested fix:**
- Make "Attack it" apply only to the selected squads, and confirm when the target is the enemy keep.
- Make Retreat pull units out, not kill them.
- Make "Finish now" end the battle.
- Show the Auto odds before Command is chosen, for example "Auto: 100%. Command at your own risk."
- Review whether manual play is balanced against Auto.

### 3. Diplomacy and war actions fail silently

**What happened:**
- Offer pact did nothing when the player had 0 decrees or not enough gold (23, 63).
- Declare war at 300 gold did nothing with 124 gold (65).
- Offer white peace closed the window with no reply (288).

**Steps:** 22-23, 62-65, 287-288

**Screenshots:** shots/step-023.jpg, shots/step-063.jpg, shots/step-065.jpg, shots/step-288.jpg

**Suggested fix:**
- Grey out unaffordable actions and name the missing resource.
- Always show a result message, such as "Saba refuses: war score too low".

### 4. Naval play is effectively unreachable

**What happened:**
- The player scrolled the whole Units list in Samaria, Megiddo and Hazor and found no ship (82-117, 136-139).
- Nothing said which cities are coastal or which tech unlocks ships.
- The "Coastal" tag was only discovered on the Jerusalem city panel at step 146.
- A Frigate was finally bought at 272-273, but the player never used a ship at sea.

**Steps:** 82-88, 107-117, 136-139, 144-148

**Screenshots:** shots/step-117.jpg

**Suggested fix:**
- Show ships greyed out in the build list for inland cities, with "Needs a coastal city" or "Needs <tech>".
- Mark coastal cities on the map or in the Cities list.

### 5. There is no full tech tree view

**What happened:**
- Era tabs did nothing visible (13, 50-51).
- Scrolling showed only boosts and era goals (54).
- Tapping a tech card or long-pressing a tech name did nothing (154-157), so the player went to the glossary to read about a tech (158-164).
- For a veteran this is a core screen.

**Steps:** 11-13, 47-58, 152-158

**Screenshots:** shots/step-055.jpg

**Suggested fix:**
- Make era tabs list every tech in that era, with prerequisites.
- Make tapping a tech open its details, with a glossary link.

**Unclear from the diary:** the tab may have switched without any visible change. The screenshot should be checked.

### 6. The late game is empty

**What happened:**
- From turns 618 to 645 (steps 391-416), almost every step was End Turn, broken only by gold-for-stability event cards.
- Gold piled up to about 30k with little to spend it on.
- The player had 18 ruler attribute points that can only be spent one tap at a time (390).
- Earlier, idle-city prompts came up every turn (342-366) until the player found Always.

**Steps:** 390-416

**Screenshots:** shots/step-417.jpg

**Suggested fix:**
- Add a bulk spend for attribute points.
- Add late-game goals and gold sinks.
- Make Always more discoverable, or default it on after a few uses.

### 7. Early game is slow to reach any action, and rebuilding is punishing

**What happened:**
- War cost 300 gold at an income of +1 per turn (65, 70).
- After the first wipe-out, one archer regiment took 24 turns to build, or 539 gold to buy at +17 per turn, and the advisor still recommended it (127).

**Steps:** 64-70, 126-127

**Screenshots:** shots/step-065.jpg, shots/step-070.jpg, shots/step-127.jpg

**Suggested fix:**
- Add an early barbarian or raid fight to introduce battles.
- Have the advisor warn about, or avoid, builds that take 20 or more turns.

### 8. New cities are dead Outposts for up to 29 turns, unannounced

**What happened:**
- Best site and the settler flow never mentioned the Outpost stage (109, 187).
- The city panel still offers "Open the city" even though the city cannot build anything.

**Steps:** 109, 185-187

**Screenshots:** shots/step-187.jpg

**Suggested fix:**
- Show the Outpost duration in Best site and the settler preview.
- Change "Open the city" to show the turns until the city can build.

### 9. Buy pricing is opaque

**What happened:**
- A Bank cost 2,324 gold to buy against an income of +303 per turn, more than double the Frigate (264).
- The Frigate's buy price fell from 1,122 to 929 between turns with no reason given (271).
- A Harbor at 149/144 production still showed "6 turns" (264).

**Steps:** 257-264, 271

**Screenshots:** shots/step-264.jpg

**Suggested fix:**
- Show the price breakdown in the buy confirm, including any decay after recent buys.
- Fix the turns-remaining display for items that are already complete.

### 10. The camera jumps to an unrelated region after a March order

**What happened:** After confirming a march from Jerusalem to Shechem, the camera jumped to Sinope and Zela, far from both.

**Steps:** 197-198

**Screenshots:** shots/step-198.jpg

**Suggested fix:** Keep the camera on the route or follow the army.

## 5. Bugs

1. **Forecast strength inconsistency at Zafar** (297, 314, 331)
   - Gunpowder or modern save, at war with Saba.
   - Select an army next to Zafar, choose March, then Attack. Note the scouts' 11,920-17,880 and "Likely win".
   - Fight the battle. The report shows 44,900.
   - Two turns later, March a new army at Zafar. The preview shows 294,900 and "Hopeless".

2. **Siege odds flip against a smaller army** (129-133)
   - Bronze save, with Jerusalem under siege by Pontus.
   - On turn N, 3,000 militia face 33,340 attackers and the preview says "Auto holds 100%".
   - End turn. Now 3,000 militia face 30,220 attackers and the preview says "0% Hopeless, Numbers x0.10".

3. **"Attack it" sends the whole army and it is wiped out unseen** (100-103)
   - In a siege defence on Command, tap Enemy, then "Attack it" on the expedition camp.
   - Within about 40 seconds of battle clock the army drops from 98% to 0%.
   - The banner changes from 1 to 2 base buildings.
   - The camp popup stays on top of the "army is gone" dialog.

4. **Retreat leads to "Your army is gone", with readouts that disagree** (309-311)
   - In the modern Zafar siege, select the last Rifle Infantry squad and tap Retreat.

7. **Modern army enters battle as Pikemen** (308)
   - A modern-age 10k army attacking Zafar fields a Pikemen squad alongside Rifle Infantry.
   - This may be intended for mixed armies. It is unclear from the diary.

8. **No result screen after an Auto fight** (106, 124-125, 129-130)

9. **Silent failure on unaffordable diplomacy actions** (22-23, 62-65)

10. **White peace offer gives no reply** (287-288)

11. **Stale "Already fighting" text** (356-357)

12. **City name mismatch** (172-174, 183-184)

13. **Queue dropdown shows no list** (45-46, 259-260)

14. **Harbor shows done but still has turns remaining** (264)
    - In Jerusalem's queue, the Harbor shows 149/144 production and "6 turns".

15. **Units filter needs a second tap** (83-84)
    - In Samaria's production, the first tap on Units switched to the Build tab without applying the filter.
    - This could be a mistimed tap.

16. **Banner X unresponsive, then the tap passes through** (141-143)
    - At the start of the classical session, the first tap on the X did nothing. The second opened a tile panel underneath.
    - This is unclear: the banner may have closed late after the first tap.

The extra enemy buildings mid-battle (9 to 10 at step 307, 1 to 2 at step 102) may be intended reinforcements. They need a check.

**Player errors, not game bugs:** step 122 was a missed tap on empty space, and step 303 tapped the player's own building.

## 6. What was good

- **Setup and tutorial:** short and clear (1-10).
- **Event cards:** clearly stated trade-offs, and the gold change shows immediately (20, 67, 69, 206, 392, 406, 410, 415).
- **Era goals:** readable and motivating, with a tracker (78, 121, 166, 278, 292, 368).
- **World Congress:** a clear panel, and the vote tally updates (250-252, 336-338).
- **Buying with gold:** the confirm step explains the 40% price rise. The clutch archer buy at 202-204 was a high point.
- **Threat alerts:** Defend and Send an army lead neatly into the city Defense tab and a march (191-193).
- **Diplomacy details:** the Why? opinion breakdown, the peace screen's refusal reasons, the tribute card and paid white peace all worked (286-287, 335, 353, 400-402).
- **Speeding up city management:** Advisor builds, Always and Best site (173, 283-284, 366).
- **Governors and laws:** both panels are tidy (178-180, 245-248, 379-385).
- **Glossary:** fast search and useful cross links (28-31, 159-164).
- **Save and load:** both worked, and autosave is reliable throughout.
- **Battle onboarding:** the briefing, deploy screen with formations, three-step tips, and the Enemy jump button are all well done (93-101, 226-233). The gunpowder loss report was clear (241-242).
- **Turn speed:** fast at every era.

## 7. Coverage

**Played well:**
- Start and setup.
- Cities and builds.
- Events, era goals and the World Congress.
- Economy and gold.
- Diplomacy, including pacts, peace and tribute.
- Speed feel.
- Glossary.
- Wars, at the strategic level.
- RTS battles: three commanded and several Auto.

**Only glanced at:**
- **Save and load:** tested once, on turn 2. Late-game saves and save size were not checked.
- **Map:** pinch, pan and fog were seen. The close view was never found.
- **Tech tree:** only the short list and landmark picks were used.
- **Units:** only March was used. Merging, splitting and stacking were never tried.
- **Independents:** one trading post choice at 267.
- **Raids:** not clearly seen.

**Missed:**
- **Naval:** no ship ever moved, transported troops or fought.
- **RTS workers and economy:** 0 laborers were available (105).
- **Offensive siege tools and sieges with siege units.**
- **Winning a manual battle:** never achieved.

**Next run should aim at:**

1. Building a ship in a known coastal city and taking it to sea, including a naval battle and a troop transport.
2. A commanded battle with favourable odds and laborers, to test workers and whether manual play can win at all.
3. Re-checking forecast consistency by recording Auto odds before and after battles.
4. Searching for a full tech tree and the close view, with screenshots of the era tabs.
5. A mid-game load and save.
6. Splitting and merging armies.
