# Human playtest: builder

# Playtest Report: Builder Persona, Phone 844x390

## 1. Summary

A peaceful, economy-focused player went through six era sessions (start, bronze, classical, gunpowder, modern, information) over 197 steps. The opening was the best part. Setup and the tutorial were smooth, founding the first village on a +3 food river site was satisfying, and events and the Empire screen gave the player plenty of numbers to read. From the classical session onward, the builder loop kept stalling:

- **Settlers stuck:** Settlers sat at "1 turn left" with progress far above cost (309/117, 450/140, 456/140) across three sessions.
- **Era economies too weak:** later-era research needed 957 turns, and the capital stayed size 1 with 3 production.
- **Ruler panel confusing:** virtue points could not be spent.

The player never found save/load or a glossary. It also never reached any war, RTS battle or naval content, because a long exploration march found nothing and the army began to starve. No page errors were recorded.

**The three things that matter most:**

1. **Settlers do not complete, and nothing explains why.** This was seen in the classical, gunpowder and modern sessions (steps 104, 125, 131, 160). For a builder this blocks the core expansion loop.
2. **Later-era starts cannot keep pace with era costs.** Research showed 18/5760 at +6 science per turn (step 138). There were 25 builds flagged "slow" at 3 production (step 131), the capital stayed size 1 (step 162), and gold was flat or falling. Later eras feel dead on arrival.
3. **Key systems were not discoverable on a phone.** Save/load, help and glossary were never found (steps 34 to 47). Virtue picks had no visible confirm (steps 110 to 120). Panels and the turn report covered End Turn and other buttons (steps 35, 95).

## 3. Fun curve

- Founding Kish on a +3 food river tile (step 16) and queuing Settlers (step 18) felt good.

**Steps 25 to 50 (fun 3, frustration rising to 2): searching for missing features.**
- Fun dipped back to 3 once the turn loop settled into reading screens.
- Frustration rose to 2 at step 35 and stayed there. The player hunted for save/load and help, found only Settings with End Turn overlapping it, and learned Settlers would take 32 turns (step 42).

- The bronze session was mostly an exploration march.

- That level held through step 98 while the army headed home starving and gold dropped. The only break was step 88 (fun 3, frustration 2), a fort-level event the builder liked.

**Steps 100 to 126 (frustration 3): virtues and stuck Settlers.**
- The classical session started neutral.
- Frustration climbed to 3 at step 112 when virtue points could not be spent.
- It stayed at 3 through step 126 as Settlers stayed at "1 turn left" for four turns.

**Steps 127 to 153: the second low point.**
- Settlers showed 450/140 and still stuck (step 131).

**Peak:** steps 16 to 24.

## 4. Top issues (ranked by harm to a real player)

### 1. Settlers never finish despite progress over cost
- **What happened:** the Build tab showed Settlers 309/117 with "1 turn left" (step 104). It stayed that way across end turns 105 to 108 (steps 121 to 125). The same thing appeared at 450/140 in the gunpowder session (step 131) and 456/140 in the modern session (step 160). In gunpowder they eventually completed after about five end turns (step 142). The information-age summary mentions "settlers waiting idle" (step 197).
- **Screenshots:** shots/step-104.jpg, shots/step-125.jpg, shots/step-131.jpg, shots/step-160.jpg
- **Unclear:** the capital was size 1 (step 162). If Settlers need a second population point to spend, the stall may be by design, and the real fault is that the UI says "1 turn left" with no reason given.
- **Fix:**
  - If a pop or other requirement blocks completion, show it plainly on the build row, for example "Waiting: city needs size 2."
  - Do not show "1 turn left" for a blocked item.
  - Cap or carry over overflow production instead of letting it pile up far past cost.
  - If there is no such requirement, this is a completion bug and needs fixing.

### 2. Later-era sessions start with an economy that cannot use the era's content
- **What happened:**
  - Research showed Gunpowder Weapons at 18/5760 with +6 science, about 957 turns (step 138).
  - "25 builds are slow at 3 production" (step 131).
  - The cheapest building was 22 to 30 turns away (steps 147, 151).
  - The capital stayed size 1 (step 162).
  - Gold fell or sat flat (steps 125, 192 to 196).
- **Effect:** a builder has nothing meaningful to plan.
- **Screenshot:** shots/step-138.jpg
- **Fix:**
  - Give era-start scenarios an empire scaled to the era: several cities, larger populations, and production and science that can finish a tech in roughly 10 to 30 turns.
  - Alternatively, scale tech costs to the player's actual output.

### 3. Exploration is empty, and war, RTS and naval content never surface
- **What happened:** in the bronze session the army marched for many turns (steps 52 to 98) and found no coast, enemies or battles. It ran out of supply and started to starve (steps 81, 95). No RTS battle or naval content was reached in any of the six sessions.
- **Persona note:** a builder avoids war by design. Even so, a nearby neighbour or coast would normally appear within that many turns, and the only contact came very late (step 170).
- **Possible label confusion:** the march targets "Mamoré", "Guaporé" and "Madre de Dios" (steps 71, 77, 84) are real rivers or regions. The player treated them as peoples to meet. It is unclear whether the game labels these in a way that suggests they are peoples.
- **Screenshots:** shots/step-062.jpg, shots/step-081.jpg, shots/step-095.jpg
- **Fix:**
  - Place at least one neighbour or independent and a coastline within a few turns' march of the start in era scenarios.
  - Label march targets by type (region, river, people).
  - Warn before an order would take an army past its supply range.

### 4. Save/load and help cannot be found
- **What happened:** Menu opens Settings directly. Scrolling Settings showed no save option (steps 34 to 39). Legacy and Log were checked without success (steps 44 to 47). A phone player who cannot find save will lose progress.
- **Screenshots:** shots/step-035.jpg, shots/step-047.jpg
- **Fix:**
  - Make the menu button open a small menu with Save, Load, Help/Glossary and Settings.
  - Alternatively, add clearly labelled Save and Help entries at the top of Settings.

### 5. Virtue points cannot be spent
- **What happened:** tapping the Founder virtue only highlighted it, and the counter stayed at 4 virtue points (steps 110, 111, 115). Scrolling found no confirm (steps 117, 118). Tapping the Master Builder tier gave no explanation of why it was locked (step 119). Attribute plus buttons did work (step 114).
- **Unclear:** the player's early claim that the plus buttons failed (step 112) was probably a missed tap at step 108 or 109.
- **Screenshots:** shots/step-112.jpg, shots/step-116.jpg, shots/step-120.jpg
- **Fix:**
  - Show a visible "Adopt (1 point)" button when a virtue is selected, kept in view on a 390px-tall screen.
  - Show each tile's cost.
  - Explain locked tiers, for example "Requires Founder."

### 6. Panels and sheets cover core buttons on the phone
- **What happened:**
  - End Turn overlaps the Settings panel (step 35).
  - The turn report sheet overlaps End Turn and the "Army idle" button (step 95), which forced repeated closing (steps 58, 96, 98, 106, 169).
  - The city panel covers half the map, and its warning text is cut off behind a "More" link (step 104).
- **Screenshots:** shots/step-035.jpg, shots/step-095.jpg, shots/step-104.jpg
- **Fix:**
  - Hide the floating End Turn and idle buttons while a full panel or sheet is open.
  - Make the turn report collapse to a small toast by default.

### 7. Build list is unit-heavy and the tabs are cramped
- **What happened:** the player scrolled the build list six times looking for cheap economy buildings among units (steps 143 to 148). It then tapped Citizens by mistake instead of Buildings (step 150); these tabs sit close together at y=124.
- **Screenshots:** none at these steps.
- **Fix:**
  - Default the build chooser to the Buildings tab, or sort by turns-to-complete.
  - Widen the tab hit areas on phone.

### 8. Late game has little to decide, and gold and food changes are opaque
- **What happened:** steps 182 to 196 were almost entirely End Turn. Gold moved without income (flat at 105 and later 45, steps 192 to 196). Food dropped from 44 to 25 in one turn with no stated cause (step 197).
- **Fix:**
  - Add a per-turn gold and food breakdown with change reasons in the turn report.
  - Prompt the player with suggested actions when cities are idle.

## 5. Bugs

**1. Settlers stuck at "1 turn left" with progress above cost** (likely bug, or missing requirement message)
- Start the classical session.
- Tap Cities (770,148), then the capital, then the city, then the Build tab (steps 100 to 103). It shows Settlers 309/117, 1 turn left.
- End turn three or more times (steps 121 to 123) and reopen Cities (step 124). Settlers are still at 1 turn left.
- Reproduced in gunpowder (steps 127 to 131, 450/140) and modern (steps 156 to 160, 456/140).

**2. Virtue cannot be adopted**
- Classical session: open Ruler (770,100) and tap the Founder virtue (495,270) at steps 110, 111 and 115. The tile highlights, but the virtue count stays at 4.
- Scrolling the panel (step 117) and tapping again (step 118) changes nothing.
- This could be a hidden confirm button rather than broken logic, so it needs checking against the screenshots.

**3. Research cost unreachable** (balance bug)
- Gunpowder session: open Research (770,192) at step 137.
- It shows 18/5760 at +6 per turn, about 957 turns.

**4. End Turn overlaps the Settings panel** (layout)
- Start session: tap Menu (770,378) at step 34. End Turn covers panel content (step 35).

**5. Turn report sheet overlaps End Turn and Army idle** (layout)
- Bronze session: end turn while an army is marching (steps 93, 94). The report sheet covers both buttons (step 95).

**6. March tile taps ignored** (unclear)
- Bronze session: select the army, open March (95,307), then tap a nearby tile (470,200). The game reports the army is already there (steps 52 to 54).
- At step 76, a tap at the fog edge "did nothing."
- These may be missed taps or tiles that cannot be selected, so they need screenshot review.

**7. Closing the Log appears to open Legacy** (probably not a bug)
- Step 44 opened Legacy, step 45 opened Log on top of it, and closing Log at step 46 revealed Legacy (step 47).
- Most likely the panels were stacked and Legacy was never closed.
- Worth confirming that opening a side panel replaces, rather than stacks on, the current one.

**8. Food drops from 44 to 25 in one turn** (unclear)
- Reported in the final summary (step 197) after queuing Storage Pits and Granary and ending many turns. There is no screenshot and the cause is unknown.

## 6. What was good

- **Setup:** fast and clear, four taps to start (steps 1 to 4).
- **Tutorial:** informative, with pages on villages, power pools, buildings and war (steps 5 to 11).
- **First city:** founding it on a site with visible food and river bonuses felt rewarding (step 16), and it was the high point of the run.
- **Event choices:** frequent and phrased as real gold trade-offs that suited a careful builder (for example steps 25, 133, 140, 164).
- **Cities list:** clear, showing Settlers and growth timers at a glance (step 42).
- **Army panel:** shows supply drain and turns until starving clearly (step 62).
- **Peoples screen:** with no contacts yet, it explains how to meet others (step 65, praise note).
- **Diplomacy:** the pact worked end to end. The cost was clear (150 gold) and opinion feedback was immediate (steps 170 to 173).
- **Attribute spending:** works once found (step 114).
- **Building queues:** Storage Pits and Granary queued cleanly from the Buildings filter (steps 175 to 180).
