## stone

The Warband is free at the start and Hunters come with Hunting Bands, a root tech, so infantry and ranged are both reachable at once. The stone age has no cavalry unit, so the triangle here is only infantry versus ranged until horses appear in a later age. Ladder Crews sit behind Copper Working for tools and the climb toward walls. Dugout Canoes and the Canoe Landing go with Star Lore, whose coastal boost and navigation theme fit, though Pottery or Agriculture could also carry early boats. The Shaman and the Stone Circle go with Sacred Kingship as the ritual and religious tech. The Earthwork Ditch goes with Chiefdoms as organized communal labor. Storage Pits pair with Pottery for food storage, and Trail Cairns with The Wheel, which already extends supply range. Every tech now unlocks something: Agriculture and Animal Husbandry keep the Farm and Pasture, and Proto-Writing keeps the Tablet House. The Quarry, Lumber camp, Fishing boats and Plantation improvements point to tech ids that are not in this age's data and should be checked against the later ages. The wonders have no tech field, so none were gated.

## bronze

Spearmen are free at the start. Chariots hang on The Wheel from the stone age, so most nations field infantry and cavalry from turn one. Archers arrive with Composite Bow, the second military tech, which keeps the counter triangle playable early. The Battering Ram and Barracks share Bronze Casting as the age's metalworking military step. The War Galley sits on Early Astronomy, since its boost favors coasts and star navigation guided early sailors. Bronze Trade Routes would also be a reasonable home, but it already gates fishing boats. The Palisade and Baggage Train go to Mudbrick Roads (mudbrick walls, supply range). The Road Post goes to Scribal Bureaucracy, read as royal messenger stations. The Shrine goes to Code of Laws as a Culture & Order building. Only Cuneiform Records is left empty, and the Great Pyramids are the suggested gate. Solomon's Temple could alternatively be gated by a governance tech.

## classical

Swordsmen are free at the start of the age. Composite Archers sit on the bronze age Composite Bow, which is a prerequisite of Iron Weapons, so nearly every classical nation has them at once. Heavy Cavalry comes with Iron Weapons, the first military tech of the age, for iron armour and lances. This keeps all three core classes reachable from the start or the first tech of the military line. The Ballista goes with Siege Engineering, and Engineers go with Paved Roads, after the Roman road and camp builders. The Trireme goes with Minted Coinage, since Athenian silver paid for its fleet. That spreads the naval unlock away from Silk Road Trade, which already holds the Harbor. All building tiers already have sensible techs. The Temple on Civic Assemblies is a little loose but fits the civic and religious life of the polis, so I left it. Provincial Administration unlocks nothing, so gating the Colosseum there fits Roman provincial public works. Other ungated wonders could also be tied to techs: the Great Library to Natural Philosophy and the Lighthouse to Silk Road Trade.

## kingdoms

Pikemen are free at the start. Longbowmen come with Feudal Levies, the first military tech, since the longbow was a levy weapon. Knights come with Feudal Charters, the first governance tech, whose boost is owning horses; the knight was the fief-holding mounted vassal. This means all three core classes are reachable from the first tech of their lines. Trebuchets sit on Plate Armor as the late high-medieval military tech, which is a thematic stretch but keeps the military line from going empty. Longships are early medieval (8th century), so they are free at the start, and the Shipyard building stays on Optics. Pioneers go to Postal Relay because they build and maintain roads. All existing building tiers looked sensible and are left unchanged: Farm Estate on Feudal Charters (manorialism), Bazaar on Guild Charters, Scriptorium on Royal Chancery (the chancery as a writing office), Cathedral on Scholastic Method, Highway on Stone Bridges. Banking Houses is left empty, and the Grand Bazaar wonder is suggested for it. The Forbidden City wonder is also ungated and would fit Royal Chancery, given its faster decrees.

## gunpowder

Musketeers are free at the start of the age. Dragoons come with Standing Armies, the second military tech, so infantry and cavalry are always reachable early and the counter triangle stays playable. Field Cannon sits on Gunpowder Weapons beside the Star Fort. Riflemen go to Calculus, since precise ballistics fits rifled fire, though rifles are historically somewhat late for this age. The Frigate goes to Joint-Stock Companies, after the armed East India fleets, which leaves Colonial Trade with the Naval Base. Sappers fit Turnpike Roads as road and field engineering. The Coal Plant moved from Calculus to Canal Locks, because canals like the Bridgewater were built to carry coal, and that also takes load off Calculus. The two governance techs unlock nothing. Versailles suits the court centralisation of Bureaucratic Reform. The Bank could move to Constitutional Law, echoing the Bank of England after 1688, but that is optional.

## modern

Rifle Infantry is free at the start. Machine Gun Teams come with Industrial Capital, the first economy tech and a prerequisite of Mechanized Warfare, and Tanks come with Mechanized Warfare, the first military tech, so all three core classes appear early and the triangle stays playable. Artillery is tied to Rail Networks (railway guns and the shell supply of the Great War), Destroyers to Mass Production (serial steel hulls), and both Fighters and AA Guns to Combined Arms, since air power and air defense arrived as one doctrine. Putting AA Guns on Computing (fire control directors) was considered but rejected because it sits deep in the science line. The Carrier Dock moves from Mechanized Warfare to Combined Arms because carriers only make sense once naval aviation exists. All other building tiers already look sensible. Highway Systems and Mass Politics unlock no unit or tier and rely on their existing effects.

## information

Mechanized Infantry and Main Battle Tanks are available at the start of the age, since modern tanks predate it. ATGM Teams come with Precision Guidance, the first military tech of the age, so all three core classes are reachable early and the counter triangle stays playable. Rocket Artillery uses the modern-age Combined Arms, as MLRS systems date from the 1980s. The Guided-Missile Destroyer uses the modern-age Computing, after the computer-driven Aegis systems. Strike Drones fit Unmanned Systems. The SAM Battery goes to Telecom Networks, standing in for networked radar and air defense, which also spreads unlocks onto the infrastructure line. All existing building tiers already point to sensible techs of this age, so none were changed. Global Institutions was the only empty tech. The World Wide Web could alternatively sit on Telecom Networks and the Global Seed Vault on Genomics, but only the most fitting gate is suggested.
