# Classical Europe — five 2D reference sheets

Active source: plans/art-image-spec-v3.md. No Blender work.
Included: roofscape, Roman temple, Roman aqueduct arch, materials palette, northern Celtic hillfort hall variant.
Roofscape uses the previous street image as a visual reference and preserves nine houses. Landmarks combine seven panels. Aqueduct shows three arches in one tier.

These are concept references, not separate final 4096-pixel views. Validate dimensions, projections and agreement between views before modelling. Roofscape remains somewhat oblique, and its 90/900 labels are illustrative rather than exact pixel exports. Temple scale label placement does not establish exact total height; Celtic ground-bank layout and roof proportions vary across panels. Aqueduct channel is drawn uncovered. Material swatches are reference art, not seamless PBR maps.
