# Wave 7 design references

These sheets were generated with the image-generation tool and visually inspected
before their corresponding Blender meshes were authored. They are design
references only. Model textures are original procedural materials; no pixels
from a generated sheet are sampled into the atlas.

- `battle-terrain.png`: river banks and modular river pieces, a stepping-stone
  ford, timber trestle, stone arch and steel truss bridges, including damaged
  and destroyed states. Written dimensions and actual socket spans take priority.
- `vegetation.png`: nine named pieces per conifer, tropical and cold battle kit,
  including three tree sizes, stump, felled, bush, rocks and ground vegetation.
- `raid-landing-props.png`: sacks and amphora, banner/arrow exit, scorched field,
  ancient galley with gangplank, ship's boats with an offshore carrack, and an
  open-bow Modern landing craft with its ramp lowered. The earlier draft omitted
  the carrack and used the wrong Modern landing structure, so it was replaced.

All meshes use the project's low-poly, muted natural palette and authored
LOD0/1/2. The sheets guide recognisable silhouette, construction, materials
and historical form; small decorative details are simplified to the budgets.
