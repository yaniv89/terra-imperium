## How the AI playtester works (for the next run)
`.github/workflows/claude-content.yml`, job `human-playtest`, script `scripts/ai/human-playtest.mjs`.
Inputs: persona, scene (`campaign` or `battle`), limit (steps), max_usd, slices (ages), size, lang.
Dispatch with `mcp__github__actions_run_trigger` on ref `claude/integration`. Results land on branches
`ai/human-playtest-<run_id>`. One queue per job, persona and scene; runs of the same persona wait.
Costs seen: about $0.01 a step; campaign 200 to 300 steps $1.7 to $3.4; battle runs $1.3 to $2.4.
